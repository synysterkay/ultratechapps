## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Scanning for Red Flags

1.  **Letting Hope Override Data:** The biggest pitfall is dismissing clear AI-identified patterns because you're emotionally invested. If the scan shows high-risk markers, trust the objective analysis over wishful thinking.
2.  **Scanning in Isolation:** One ambiguous text might not be a red flag, but a pattern of them is. Avoid analyzing single events without using the Pattern Tracker to see the bigger picture. Context is king in behavioral analysis.
3.  **Ignoring Your Own Input:** The AI's accuracy depends on the quality of information you provide. Being vague or downplaying events ("it wasn't that bad") will yield less useful results. Be brutally honest in your descriptions.
4.  **Confronting a Partner with the "Report":** Using the app's output as ammunition in an argument can escalate conflict. This tool is for your private clarity and education, not a weapon. Use the insights to inform your boundaries, not to accuse.
5.  **Not Scanning Early Interactions:** Don't wait for a major incident. Use the app to analyze intense early romance (potential love bombing) or subtle put-downs. Proactive scanning can save you months of heartache.
