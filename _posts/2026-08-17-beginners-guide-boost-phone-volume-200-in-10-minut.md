#### Step 1: Turn Off Automatic Volume Limiting

Most phones have a built-in volume limiter to protect your hearing. This is often set to a conservative level. Go to **Settings > Sounds & Vibration > Volume**, and look for "Media volume limit" or "Volume limiter." Turn it off. You'll notice an immediate 10-15% increase in max volume.

**What you'll see:** A toggle switch that you can flip off. If it's not there, search "volume limit" in your settings.

**Common error:** Some users forget to also disable the "Absolute volume" option in Developer Options (if enabled), which can cause inconsistent volume levels with Bluetooth devices.

#### Step 2: Adjust Individual App Volume

Many apps have their own volume controls that are separate from the system volume. For example, YouTube Music, Spotify, and podcast apps often have an internal equalizer. Open your music app, go to its settings, and ensure the volume is set to 100%. Also, check if there's a "Loudness normalization" option—turn it OFF, as it can reduce volume for consistency.

**What you'll see:** A volume slider or a toggle for "Normalize volume." Turn off normalization.

**Common error:** Users often overlook this and think the system volume is the only control.
