### Why Traditional Equalizer Settings Fail

The built-in equalizers on most phones are garbage. Here's why:

1. **Limited bands** - Most phones offer only 5 bands. That's like trying to paint a masterpiece with 5 colors. Professional EQs use 10, 15, or 31 bands for precision.

2. **Presets are generic** - "Rock," "Pop," "Jazz" presets are marketing labels, not audio science. They rarely match the actual frequency needs of specific songs.

3. **No visual feedback** - You're adjusting sliders blindly. Without seeing a frequency response curve, you're guessing.

4. **Reset after switching apps** - Many phones revert to default settings when you change audio sources, forcing you to reconfigure every time.
