## The Science of Sound: How Equalizers Work

Before diving into specific settings, let's understand the basics. An equalizer (EQ) adjusts the balance between frequency components of an audio signal. Common frequency bands include:

- **Bass (20-250 Hz):** Adds depth and punch.
- **Midrange (250-4 kHz):** Affects vocals and instruments.
- **Treble (4-20 kHz):** Adds clarity and brightness.

When you boost volume beyond the default limit, you risk clipping and distortion. That's where a dedicated volume booster app comes in—it uses advanced algorithms to amplify audio without compromising quality.
