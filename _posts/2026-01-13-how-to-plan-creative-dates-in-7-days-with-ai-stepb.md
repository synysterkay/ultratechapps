### 5 Common Date-Planning Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **The Surprise Trap:** Planning a complete surprise date can backfire if your partner has other commitments or isn't in the mood for that activity. Using SoulPlan's collaborative scheduling ensures buy-in and excitement from both sides.
2.  **Overcomplicating & Overspending:** A creative date doesn't mean expensive or elaborate. The AI in SoulPlan can generate fantastic ideas for low or no budget. Avoid the pitfall of equating cost with romance.
3.  **Not Accounting for Energy Levels:** Scheduling a high-energy adventure date after a grueling workweek often leads to cancellation. Use the mood selector ("Cozy," "Chill") in the app to match dates to your real-life energy.
4.  **Forgetting the Follow-Through:** The magic of a great date can fade if you don't acknowledge it. Skipping the post-date reflection in the app misses a chance to solidify the positive memory and emotional connection.
