### Common Mistakes to Avoid

*   **Poor Audio Quality:** Placing your phone too far away or in a noisy environment results in a garbled transcript. Position it centrally on the table for best results.
*   **Forgetting to Tag:** Skipping the tagging step turns your app into a digital junk drawer. Spend 10 seconds tagging each recording by project or client for effortless retrieval later.
*   **Not Verifying Action Items:** While the AI is excellent, always quickly scan the extracted action items for context. It ensures "look into" becomes a clear, assignable task.
