The biggest AI news this month isn't a new language model—it's a fundamental shift in application. Advanced algorithms are now capable of synthesizing vast datasets—from local event calendars and weather patterns to user reviews and travel blogs—to generate recommendations that feel tailor-made for an individual couple's story.

This goes beyond simple filtering. Modern AI systems use collaborative filtering and natural language processing to understand the *emotional* context behind a search.
