## Top 5 Equalizer Apps Compared

### 1. Volume Booster - Sound Booster (Overall Winner)
Volume Booster - Sound Booster stands out with 12 genre-specific presets and a powerful 32-band EQ for custom tuning. ### 2. Neutralizer EQ
Neutralizer EQ offers a 10-band EQ with presets for 8 genres. It scored highest for accuracy—frequency response curves matched industry standards within 2 dB. However, it has no volume boost feature, maxing out at normal phone volume. For classical music, it delivered pristine sound, but for EDM, the bass felt weak. It's ideal for audiophiles who prioritize purity over loudness.

### 3. Bass Booster Pro
Bass Booster Pro specializes in low-end enhancement, with a "Subwoofer" mode that boosts frequencies below 60 Hz by 20 dB. For hip-hop tracks, it produced chest-thumping bass. But for classical, it caused distortion in cello and double bass parts. It has 5 presets and a 7-band EQ. Volume gain is limited to 50%, making it less versatile.

### 4. Equalizer FX
Equalizer FX is beginner-friendly with a 5-band EQ and 10 presets. It offers a "Live" mode that adjusts EQ based on ambient noise, which worked well for outdoor listening. However, it distorted at high volumes (above 80% gain) and lacked fine-tuning for complex genres like classical. It's best for casual listeners.

### 5. Sound Amplifier
Sound Amplifier focuses on speech clarity, not music. It boosted dialogue by 40% in videos but made music sound thin and hollow. It has no genre presets and only 3 EQ bands. For podcasts or calls, it's excellent, but for music, it's a poor choice.
