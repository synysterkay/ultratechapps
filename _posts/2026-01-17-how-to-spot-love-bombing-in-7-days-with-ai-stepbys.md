## Why This Tutorial Matters: The Hidden Danger of Intense Romance

### Step 2: Define Your Baseline for "Normal"

What does healthy, gradual affection look like to you? Jot down a few notes. Genuine connection typically involves mutual sharing, respect for pace, and balanced communication. Love bombing, in contrast, often features one-sided idolization, future-faking (making premature long-term plans), and constant contact that disregards your space. ### Step 7: Synthesize Findings and Take Action

By Day 7, you have three key data points: your gut feeling, your manual observations, and the AI's objective reports. Synthesize them. ## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid During Your Analysis

1.  **Overreacting to a Single Data Point:** One flagged phrase like "I miss you" is not conclusive. The app detects patterns, not isolated incidents. Avoid the pitfall of ending a potential connection over one scan. Look for consistency across multiple analyses.
2.  **Ignoring Your Own Intuition:** The AI is a tool, not an oracle. If your gut screams discomfort but the app shows a low risk score, don't dismiss your feelings. The technology may not have captured non-verbal cues or tone. Use the report as a discussion point with yourself or a trusted friend.
3.  **Confronting Someone with the App Report:** Never say, "My AI app says you're love bombing me." This will likely lead to defensiveness and manipulation. Use the insights privately to guide your own behavior and boundaries. The power is in your informed response, not in an accusation.
4.  **Scanning Out of Context:** Avoid pasting angry arguments or logistical planning texts. The AI is trained on romantic and manipulative language. For best results, scan typical daily conversations where affection or persuasion is present.
5.  **Assuming All Intensity is Bad:** New relationships can be exciting and passionate! The key differentiator is respect. Genuine passion respects your boundaries, independence, and pace. The app helps you see if intensity is paired with control or disregard for your comfort.
