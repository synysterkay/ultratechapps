## The Latest AI Breakthrough: Neural Audio Processing

This week, researchers unveiled a significant leap in **real-time audio signal processing** powered by lightweight neural networks. Unlike traditional digital signal processing (DSP) that often introduces harsh clipping or unwanted artifacts when boosting volume, these new AI models work differently. They analyze the audio waveform in real-time, predicting and reconstructing the harmonic content that gets lost when sound is pushed beyond a device's hardware limits.

The core innovation lies in a technique called **generative audio inpainting**. Think of it like a smart audio engineer living inside your phone.
