### 5 Equalizer Mistakes That Ruin Your Audio

1. **Over-boosting bass (60Hz)** - Creates muddy, bloated sound that drowns other frequencies. Solution: Boost moderately and cut competing frequencies (125-250Hz).
2. **Extreme V-shape curves** - Boosting both bass and treble excessively creates a hollow mid-range. Solution: Use gentle slopes and preserve mid frequencies.
3. **Ignoring headphone differences** - Using the same EQ for all headphones. Solution: Create and label device-specific profiles in Volume Booster - Sound Booster.
4. **Setting and forgetting** - Your hearing changes daily. Solution: Weekly quick checks and minor adjustments.
5. **Copying others' settings** - Everyone's ears and gear differ. Solution: Use others' settings as starting points, then customize.
