## Common Mistakes

1. **Maxing out bass boost** – Causes distortion and can damage speakers. Solution: Keep bass boost under 70% and use EQ to fine-tune.
2. **Using bass boost for all content** – Podcasts and audiobooks become muddy. Solution: Switch to EQ presets optimized for vocals.
3. **Ignoring the equalizer** – Missing out on clarity and balance. Solution: Use EQ to cut frequencies that cause muddiness (e.g., 230Hz).
4. **Not saving presets** – Wasting time reconfiguring. Solution: Save custom presets for each use case.
5. **Forgetting to disable other EQ apps** – Conflicting settings cause weird audio. Solution: Turn off built-in equalizers in streaming apps.
