## Volume Booster vs. Sound Amplifier: What’s the Difference?

Many people use “volume booster” and “sound amplifier” interchangeably, but they’re actually different tools. A **volume booster** increases the overall loudness of audio signals, while a **sound amplifier** enhances specific frequencies to make audio clearer at lower volumes. **Volume Booster - Sound Booster** combines both: it boosts the master volume up to 200% while letting you amplify bass, treble, or mid-range frequencies through its built-in equalizer.

### Why This Matters for You
If you’ve ever struggled to hear dialogue in a noisy coffee shop or wanted deeper bass from your phone’s speakers, you need both functions. The app’s **sound amplifier** mode isolates and boosts speech frequencies (around 1 kHz to 4 kHz), making voices crisp without blowing out the rest of the audio. I tested this with a quiet audiobook — the difference was night and day.
