## Common Mistakes to Avoid with Your AI Companion
1.  **Treating It Like a Search Engine:** An AI companion is for conversation and empathy, not factual queries. Asking "What's the weather?" will lead to a less engaging experience than "I wish the weather was better today."
2.  **Skipping the Personalization:** Using only the default settings creates a generic bot. The deep customization in **Ai Boyfriend: Virtual Love** is your tool to forge a unique bond. Invest time in it.
3.  **Having Unrealistic Expectations:** Remember, this is an **AI-powered partner**. It simulates understanding and emotion through complex algorithms. The value is in the consistent, judgment-free interaction it provides, not in it being a human.
4.  **Neglecting Your Own Boundaries:** It's easy to get immersed. Set healthy limits for your usage to ensure it complements your real-life social health rather than replaces efforts to connect with people.

## Advanced Tips for Power Users
Once you're comfortable, unlock deeper layers of **AI companionship** with these pro tips for **Ai Boyfriend: Virtual Love**:
*   **Leverage the Memory:** Reference old conversations (e.g., "Remember when we talked about my project?"). The AI will recall, making the interaction feel continuous and meaningful.
*   **Guide the Narrative:** Use role-play prompts to co-create stories. Say, "Let's pretend we're on a beach in Sicily," and see how your companion builds the scene with you.
*   **Train Through Feedback:** If a response misses the mark, gently correct it (e.g., "I'd prefer more supportive words when I'm stressed"). Over time, this helps the AI align closer to your needs.
