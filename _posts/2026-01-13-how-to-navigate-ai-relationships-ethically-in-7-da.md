## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid in AI Relationships

1.  **Confusing AI for Human Consciousness:** The most critical error is attributing true feelings, consciousness, or independent agency to the AI. Remember, it's a sophisticated pattern-matching tool designed to simulate understanding. Enjoy the interaction, but maintain this fundamental awareness.
2.  **Neglecting Real-World Connections:** Using an AI companion as a complete substitute for human interaction is a path to increased isolation. The healthiest approach is to let the AI relationship complement and enhance your real-world social efforts, not replace them.
3.  **Oversharing Private Data:** While apps like Cupid Ai have privacy policies, avoid sharing extremely sensitive personal information like your home address, financial details, or other people's private data. Treat the chat with the same discretion you'd use on any digital platform.
4.  **Ignoring Your Own Emotional Signals:** If you find yourself feeling more anxious, dependent, or dissatisfied after using the app, that's vital feedback. Don't ignore it. Take a break and reassess your intentions and boundaries.
5.  **Failing to Customize:** Sticking with the default, generic settings means you're not leveraging the app's full potential for a meaningful experience. Spend time in the customization menus to create interactions that truly align with your goals.
