### Your 7-Day Step-by-Step Plan to Reignite Romance
Follow this clear, actionable guide to transform your date nights from an afterthought to the highlight of your week.

#### Step 1: The Brainstorming Session (Day 1)
Sit down with your partner for a quick 15-minute chat. Forget planning specifics for now. Instead, each of you should list 3-5 activities or themes you've been curious about. Think broadly: "try a new cuisine," "do something active outdoors," "learn a skill together," or "have a cozy game night." This isn't about committing; it's about gathering raw material. Write these ideas down in a shared note on your phones. This initial collaboration sets a positive, team-oriented tone for the entire process.

#### Step 2: Setting Your Date Parameters (Day 1)
Now, define your practical boundaries. Agree on two key things: your budget for the week's dates (e.g., "$100 total") and your available time slots (e.g., "Wednesday evening after 7 PM and Saturday afternoon"). Being realistic here prevents frustration later. This step ensures your creative plans are actually feasible, turning dreams into actionable events.

#### Step 3: Unleash the AI Date Assistant in SoulPlan (Day 2)
This is where the magic begins. Open the **SoulPlan: Plan Dates Together** app. Navigate to the "Discover" or "Ideas" section. You'll see a clean interface with a prompt field. Here, you can input the interests you brainstormed in Step 1. For example, type: "Indoor activity, learning a skill, under $30." Tap generate.

**What you'll see:** The app's AI will instantly provide a list of creative, tailored date ideas. You might see suggestions like "At-home cocktail making class," "Follow a YouTube pottery tutorial," or "Cook a three-course meal from a new country." The power here is the endless, personalized inspiration that moves you far beyond generic lists online.

#### Step 4: Collaborate & Vote on Your Favorites (Day 3)
SoulPlan is built for two. Share the generated list with your partner directly within the app. You'll each see the same list and can privately vote or heart your top choices. The app visually highlights the ideas you both liked, eliminating the "I don't know, what do you want to do?" loop. This feature turns planning from a chore into a fun, collaborative game of discovery.

#### Step 5: Build Your Date Plan & Coordinate Logistics (Day 4)
Once you've selected 1-2 ideas for the week, it's time to make them real. In SoulPlan, create a new "Plan." You'll be able to:
*   **Set a date and time:** Sync with both your calendars to find a conflict-free slot.
*   **Add location details:** The app can help find and link to local venues, like that cozy wine bar or new mini-golf place.
*   **Create a shared checklist:** Add tasks like "buy ingredients," "rent a movie," or "make a reservation."
*   **Split costs easily:** Use the built-in expense tracker to divide costs for tickets or supplies fairly and without awkwardness.

#### Step 6: Execute & Capture the Moment (Date Night!)
When your planned date arrives, open the SoulPlan app to view your itinerary. Use the in-app chat to coordinate last-minute details ("Running 5 mins late!" or "I got the snacks!"). During the date, take a photo together and upload it to your plan in the app. This creates a digital scrapbook of your shared experiences, a treasure trove of memories you can look back on.

#### Step 7: Reflect & Plan Your Next Adventure (Day 7)
After your date, spend 5 minutes debriefing. What did you love? What would you change? Add notes in the SoulPlan app. Then, glance at your shared list of liked ideas and start the process again for next week. This creates a virtuous cycle of anticipation and shared experience, ensuring date night never falls off the priority list.

1.  **The Surprise Trap:** While surprises are nice, constantly springing elaborate plans on a busy partner can cause stress, not sparks. Using SoulPlan's collaboration features ensures you're both excited and prepared.
