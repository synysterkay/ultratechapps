## **5 Common Mistakes That Damage Your Speakers**

1.  **Maxing out the boost immediately:** Jumping to 100% boost will almost certainly cause distortion and can physically damage the speaker coil. Start low and increase gradually.
2.  **Ignoring the equalizer:** A flat boost amplifies all frequencies equally, including background noise and harsh highs. Use the EQ to shape the sound for clarity.
3.  **Using poor-quality audio files:** Low-bitrate MP3s (128 kbps or lower) have less dynamic range and will distort faster when boosted. Use 320 kbps or lossless files for best results.
