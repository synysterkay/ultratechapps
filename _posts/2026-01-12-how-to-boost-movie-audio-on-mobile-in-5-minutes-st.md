### Common Mistakes to Avoid When Boosting Audio

Avoiding these pitfalls will ensure you get the best possible sound without damaging your speakers or ears.

1.  **Maxing Out the Boost Immediately:** Cranking the booster to 100% right away often causes distortion, clipping (a crackling sound), and can potentially harm your device's speakers. Always increase the level gradually until you reach a clear, loud volume without audible distortion.
2.  **Ignoring the Equalizer:** Relying solely on the volume boost is a missed opportunity. Without EQ adjustment, you might just make muddy audio louder. Using the bass booster and treble controls is essential for shaping the sound to be clear and balanced for movie content.
