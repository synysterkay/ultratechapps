## My Testing Methodology

I spent 30 days—roughly 60 hours—evaluating 5 popular volume booster and equalizer apps. I used a consistent setup: Samsung Galaxy S23 (Android 14), Sennheiser HD 560S headphones, and a Dayton Audio iMM-6 calibrated microphone. For each app, I measured:

- **Max volume boost** (dB SPL at ear level)
- **Frequency response** (20 Hz–20 kHz via REW software)
- **Distortion** (THD at 90 dB output)
- **Battery drain** (percentage per 30 minutes at 80% volume)

I played reference tracks from four genres: rock (Nirvana – “Smells Like Teen Spirit”), classical (Beethoven’s 5th), electronic (Daft Punk – “Around the World”), and podcasts (Joe Rogan Experience).
