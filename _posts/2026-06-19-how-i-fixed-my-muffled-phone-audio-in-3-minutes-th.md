## Why the Usual Fixes Fail

Most people try the obvious solutions first. You crank the volume slider to 100%. You toggle the equalizer in your music app. Maybe you even buy a louder pair of headphones. But here’s why none of that works:

- **System volume limits**: Phone manufacturers cap maximum output to prevent speaker damage. That slider is lying to you—it’s not hitting true 100%.
- **EQ presets**: Apps like Spotify have basic EQ, but they only adjust frequency balance, not overall loudness. You get more treble, not more power.
- **Headphone upgrades**: Spending $200 on headphones doesn’t fix a weak phone amp. The bottleneck is the device, not the drivers.
- **Volume booster apps (bad ones)**: Some apps simply amplify the signal, introducing clipping and distortion. You get loud, but it sounds like a blown speaker.

I tried three different “volume booster” apps before finding one that actually worked without ruining audio quality. Most of them just made everything sound like static. It was a waste of time and storage.
