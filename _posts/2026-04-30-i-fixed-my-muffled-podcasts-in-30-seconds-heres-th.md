## Why Traditional Solutions Fail

Most people try the obvious fixes first, but they rarely work:

- **Maxing out phone volume:** This only amplifies the existing audio, often introducing distortion and clipping—especially on budget smartphones.
- **Using headphones with louder drivers:** While this helps slightly, it doesn't fix the underlying audio imbalance. You're still stuck with the same muddy mix.
- **Equalizer apps:** These require manual tweaking of frequencies, which takes time and expertise. A 2022 survey by Headphonesty found that **73% of users found equalizers too complicated** to use effectively.

What you really need is a tool that **intelligently boosts quiet sections while preventing loud parts from distorting**—something that works automatically in the background.
