## 3 Common Thesis Statement Mistakes to Avoid

Even with AI help, watch out for these pitfalls that weaken your argument:

1.  **The Fact, Not a Claim:** A thesis must be arguable. "Carbon dioxide is a greenhouse gas" is a fact. "Human-produced carbon dioxide is the primary driver of modern climate change and requires immediate policy intervention" is a debatable claim.
2.  **The Vague Generalization:** Avoid words like "good," "bad," "important," or "society." Be specific. Instead of "Social media is bad for teens," try "Excessive social media use correlates with increased anxiety and reduced face-to-face communication skills in adolescents."
3.  **The Overly Broad Scope:** Don't try to solve world hunger in five pages. Narrow your focus. "A analysis of symbolism in *The Great Gatsby*" is too broad. "F. Scott Fitzgerald uses the green light and the eyes of Doctor T.J. Eckleburg in *The Great Gatsby* to critique the corruption of the American Dream" is focused and manageable.
