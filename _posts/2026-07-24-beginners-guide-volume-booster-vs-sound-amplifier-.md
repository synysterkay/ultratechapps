### Step-by-Step Tutorial: Mastering Volume Booster vs Sound Amplifier

#### Step 1: Understand the Core Difference

Volume boosting increases the digital signal before it's converted to sound. Think of it like turning up the gain on a microphone—it makes everything louder, but also amplifies background noise and can cause distortion. Sound amplification, on the other hand, increases the power of the analog signal after conversion, which is cleaner but limited by your device's hardware. Volume Booster - Sound Booster uses a hybrid approach, applying digital boosting within safe limits and then optimizing the analog output for clarity.

#### Step 3: Configure the Volume Booster Mode

Tap "Volume Booster" from the menu. You'll see a slider that goes from 0% to 200%. Start at 50%—this is a safe boost for most speakers. Play a test track and slowly increase the slider. What you'll hear: the audio gets louder, but at around 120%, you might notice a slight buzz or distortion. That's the digital boosting limit for your device. I recommend staying between 50% and 100% for general use. Common error: jumping to 200% immediately, which can cause clipping.
