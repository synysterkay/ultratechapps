## 5 Common Mistakes That Damage Speakers (And How to Avoid Them)

Even with a great tool like Volume Booster - Sound Booster, people still make errors. Here are the most common pitfalls:

**1. Boosting from Maximum Base Volume**
If your phone's volume is already at 100% and you add a +12 dB boost, you're asking for trouble. Always start with your phone's volume at 70-80% before boosting.

**2. Ignoring the Limiter**
Some users turn off the Smart Limiter thinking it's limiting their volume. In reality, it's limiting distortion. Keep it ON at all times.

**3. Using Cheap Headphones**
Low-quality earbuds can't handle high boost levels. They'll distort or even blow out. Invest in decent headphones if you plan to boost regularly.

**4. Forgetting to Switch Profiles**
Using a music profile (high bass) for phone calls can make voices sound muddy and cause speaker strain. Switch profiles based on content.

**5. Boosting for Extended Periods**
Even with safety features, prolonged high-volume listening can damage hearing. Use the boost for short bursts (e.g., a song, a video) and lower it for background listening.
