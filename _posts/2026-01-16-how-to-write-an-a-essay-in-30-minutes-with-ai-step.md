## Your Step-by-Step Guide to AI-Powered Essay Writing
Follow these seven steps to go from a blank page to a structured draft in record time.

### Step 1: Deconstruct Your Assignment Prompt
Don't just read the prompt—analyze it. Circle the action verbs (analyze, compare, argue, describe) and underline the key concepts. What is the core question being asked? Write this down in your own words. This initial clarity prevents you from going off-topic later. For example, if the prompt is "Analyze the impact of social media on political discourse," your core task is *analysis*, not just description.

Finally, run a thorough spell-check and grammar check.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI for Writing
While AI is powerful, misusing it can backfire. Avoid these pitfalls:

1.  **Passive Copy-Pasting:** The biggest mistake is copying AI output verbatim. This can lead to detection for academic dishonesty and, more importantly, means you're not learning. Always use the AI's suggestions as a draft to heavily edit, refine, and make your own.
2.  **Skipping the Brainstorming Phase:** If you feed the AI a vague or poorly thought-out prompt, you'll get a vague and generic response. The AI's output quality is directly tied to your input quality. The 5 minutes of brainstorming in Step 2 are crucial.
3.  **Neglecting Your Own Voice:** AI-generated text can sometimes sound generic. It's your job to inject your unique perspective, academic voice, and specific course knowledge. Weave in personal insights or references from your class lectures.
4.  **Forgetting to Fact-Check:** AI is a language model, not a fact-checker. It can sometimes generate plausible-sounding but incorrect information or examples. Always verify any facts, dates, names, or quotes the AI suggests before including them in your final paper.
