### Your 7-Day Step-by-Step Writing Plan
Follow this actionable plan to go from concept to completed story outline and chapters.

#### **Step 1: Define Your Core Romance Concept (Day 1)**
Start by answering three questions: What is the central romantic conflict? Who are the two main characters? What makes their love story unique? Don't overthink it. Examples: "A shy librarian inherits a vineyard and clashes with the rugged winemaker next door," or "Two rival chefs are forced to co-host a cooking show." Write this logline down—it's your North Star.

#### **Step 2: Sketch Your Main Characters (Day 2)**
Flesh out your protagonist and love interest. Give them names, appearances, careers, and a key personality trait. Most importantly, define their internal wound (a past hurt that affects their present) and what they truly desire. A character who wants love but fears abandonment is instantly more compelling than a generic "nice person."

#### **Step 4: Co-Write the First Chapter (Day 4)**
This is where the magic happens. Within LoveStory AI, you don't just read—you participate. After the AI sets the scene, you'll be given multiple choice decisions that shape the dialogue and action. Does your character respond with sarcasm or vulnerability? Your role is to guide the emotional tone and key decisions, letting the AI handle the descriptive prose and continuity. #### **Step 5: Develop Chemistry Through Dialogue (Day 5)**
Romance lives in the tension and connection between characters. Use the app's interactive dialogue trees to build this. When presented with choices, think about subtext. A choice that seems like an argument might actually be flirting. The AI will reflect the tone you choose, allowing you to craft delicious slow-burn tension or fiery confrontations. **What you'll see:** The characters' personalities solidify through their spoken words. The AI ensures their voices remain consistent, helping you explore their relationship dynamics in real-time.

#### **Step 6: Navigate the Midpoint Crisis (Day 6)**
Every great romance needs a dark moment where all seems lost. Guide your story to its midpoint crisis. In the app, this might mean making a choice that leads to a misunderstanding, a secret revealed, or an external threat. **What you'll see:** The AI will escalate the stakes based on your prior choices, creating a believable and emotionally charged low point. This collaborative process helps you avoid plot holes and ensures the crisis feels earned, not forced.

#### **Step 7: Refine and Plan the Ending (Day 7)**
Review the story you've co-created in the app. Export the text or take screenshots of key scenes. Now, step back into the role of sole author. Use this AI-generated draft as your foundation. Identify the strongest lines of dialogue and most evocative descriptions. Plan your final act: how will the characters overcome their flaws and the central conflict to earn their happy-ever-after (or happy-for-now)?

### Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI for Writing
1.  **Being Too Vague:** Telling the AI "write a romance story" will give you generic results. Always provide specific details about characters, setting, and conflict for a unique narrative.
2.  **Relinquishing All Control:** The AI is a tool, not an author. If a story direction feels off, use the app's features to make a different choice or restart from a previous point. You are the creative director.
