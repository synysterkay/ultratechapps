### Step 2: Gather Your Whole Food Ingredients
Head to the grocery store with a list of canine-friendly foods. Focus on lean proteins like chicken breast, turkey, or white fish. For carbohydrates, opt for digestible options like brown rice, sweet potato, or pumpkin. Don't forget fibrous vegetables like green beans, carrots, and broccoli. Avoid onions, garlic, grapes, and excessive fats.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid in Canine Weight Loss

**1. Eyeballing Portions:** Guessing food amounts is the top reason diets fail. A few extra kibbles or a heaping spoonful can add hundreds of hidden calories. Always use a digital scale, as enforced by the PupShape logging system.

**2. Forgetting Treat Calories:** Those training treats and table scraps count! They can easily blow your dog's entire daily calorie deficit. Log every single treat in the PupShape app to stay on track, or use low-calorie options like pieces of their regular vegetables.

**3. Changing Food Too Quickly:** A sudden switch to a new homemade diet can cause stomach upset. Always transition over 7-10 days, slowly mixing the new food with the old, allowing their digestive system to adapt.

**4. Neglecting Exercise:** Diet alone is slow and less effective. Sustainable weight loss requires a combination of controlled nutrition and increased physical activity to build lean muscle and burn fat.
