## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI for Romance

1.  **Being Too Vague:** Inputting "write a romance story" will yield generic results. Always provide specific tropes, character flaws, and desired emotional beats.
2.  **Accepting the First Output:** The first draft from any AI is just a starting point. The real value comes from iterative refinement. Don't settle; guide and re-prompt.
3.  **Neglecting Character Consistency:** As you generate different scenes, ensure character voices and motivations remain consistent. Use the app's character profiles as a constant reference.
4.  **Forgetting the "Why":** AI can generate plot, but you must supply the emotional core. Always ask yourself *why* characters fall in love. That depth must come from you.
5.  **Over-Reliance:** The tool is a collaborator, not a replacement. Your unique perspective, experiences, and voice are what will make the story special. Use the AI to augment, not automate, your creativity.
