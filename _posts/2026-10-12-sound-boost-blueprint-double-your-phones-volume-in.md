## Common Mistakes to Avoid
1. **Maxing out the booster immediately** – Start at 120% and increase gradually. Sudden jumps can distort audio.
2. **Ignoring the equalizer** – The EQ is crucial for clean sound. Without it, you'll just amplify noise.
3. **Forgetting to disable battery optimization** – Android may kill the app in the background. Go to Settings > Apps > Volume Booster > Battery > Unrestricted.
4. **Using the booster with damaged speakers** – If your speaker is blown, boosting will make it worse. Test with headphones first.
5. **Not saving presets** – You'll waste time reconfiguring every time. Save at least three presets.
