## Why Traditional Solutions Fail

You’ve probably tried some of these common fixes:
- **Third-party EQ apps:** They adjust frequencies but rarely boost overall volume beyond the OS limit. They’re like rearranging deck chairs on the Titanic.
- **External Bluetooth speakers:** They work, but they’re bulky, require charging, and defeat the purpose of a portable phone.
- **Rooting or jailbreaking:** This voids your warranty and opens your phone to security risks. Plus, it’s complicated and can brick your device.
- **Cheap “booster” apps from unknown developers:** These apps often bypass hardware protections, pushing speakers beyond safe thresholds. That’s exactly what cost me $150. They lack real-time monitoring and can cause permanent distortion.

The core issue? Most solutions either don’t work or actively harm your device. They ignore the physics of speaker hardware. A speaker has a maximum excursion—how far the cone can move without tearing. Pushing it past that limit with raw gain creates distortion and damage.
