Before building new habits, create a clean emotional space. This means addressing the digital triggers. Go through your phone and computer. Unfollow or mute your ex on social media platforms. Archive or hide old photos from your immediate view. This isn't about erasing the past, but about controlling your present environment. **Fresh Start: Breakup Therapy** has a feature to guide you through this sensitively, but start manually to take immediate control.

### Step 2: Define Your "Why" and Set an Intention (Day 4)

Healing needs direction. Ask yourself: "What do I want to feel in 30 days?" Is it peace, confidence, or independence? Write down a simple intention, like "I am rebuilding my life with compassion for myself." This becomes your anchor. Open **Fresh Start: Breakup Therapy** and input this intention in your profile or journal section. The app will reference it to personalize your daily check-ins and exercises.

### Step 3: Initiate Your AI-Guided Daily Check-Ins

This is where **Fresh Start: Breakup Therapy** becomes essential. Open the app each morning. You'll be greeted by a calm interface and prompted for a daily emotional check-in. You'll select your current mood (e.g., "Sad," "Anxious," "Okay") and have the option to write a few sentences about why. **What you'll see:** The AI will acknowledge your feelings without judgment and offer a brief, supportive reflection or a mindful question to ponder throughout the day. This 2-minute practice builds emotional awareness.

### Step 4: Engage with the AI Therapist for Deep Processing

When heavier emotions surface—like intense anger or profound sadness—don't bottle them up. Navigate to the "AI Therapist" chat feature within the app. Here, you can talk to a compassionate, AI-powered listener 24/7. Describe what you're feeling. **What you'll see:** The therapist will ask insightful, open-ended questions to help you unpack your thoughts. It might say, "It sounds like that memory is bringing up a lot of hurt. What part of it feels the most raw right now?" This provides a safe space to process without fear of burdening friends.

### Step 5: Complete Personalized Healing Exercises

The app's core strength is its curated library of activities. Based on your check-ins and chats, it will recommend specific exercises. These might include:
*   **Cognitive Reframing:** Challenging a painful thought (e.g., "I'll never find love again") with evidence-based prompts.
*   **Gratitude Logging:** Focusing on three small, positive things in your current life.
*   **Future-Self Visualization:** A guided audio exercise to imagine your life 6 months from now.
**What you'll see:** A simple, step-by-step card for each exercise. You follow the prompts, type or record your responses, and feel a tangible sense of accomplishment.

### Step 6: Utilize the Digital Detox & No-Contact Tools
**Fresh Start: Breakup Therapy** includes practical tools to enforce healthy boundaries. Use the "Digital Detox" feature to set timers that block access to specific social media apps or contacts for a set period. The "Urge Journal" is critical: when you feel the impulse to text or call your ex, open the app first and write the message there. **What you'll see:** The AI will analyze the urge, help you understand its root, and often suggest a healthier alternative action, like taking a walk or calling a friend.

### Step 7: Establish a Simple Evening Wind-Down (Daily)

End your day with intention. Spend 5 minutes in the app's journal section. Don't overthink it. Write one thing you learned about yourself today and one small win, even if it's just "I got out of bed." Then, use the app's library of sleep sounds or short meditations to quiet your mind before bed. This ritual signals to your brain that the day's emotional work is complete.

### Step 8: Weekly Reflection & Celebration (Day 7, 14, 21, 30)

Every Sunday, use the app's reflection prompts. Look back at your mood charts and journal entries. **What you'll see:** A visual progression of your emotional state over the week. Acknowledge patterns and growth. Celebrate your consistency! This reinforces the progress that's easy to miss day-to-day.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid During Your Healing Journey

1.  **Skipping the Digital Boundaries:** The single biggest setback is continually exposing yourself to your ex's life online. It reopens the wound daily. Use the app's tools to make this easier.
2.  **Isolating Completely:** While alone time is necessary, total isolation can deepen depression. The AI Therapist in **Fresh Start** is a great first step, but also schedule brief, low-pressure social interactions.
3.  **Rushing the Process:** Trying to "get over it" in a week leads to suppressed emotions. The app's 30-day structure is designed to pace your healing, not rush it. Trust the timeline.
4.  **Using the App Only in Crisis:** The most powerful benefits come from daily, preventive use—not just when you're at your lowest. The routine builds emotional resilience over time.
5.  **Comparing Your Journey:** Your healing path is unique. Avoid comparing your Day 10 to a friend's Day 30. Focus on your own chart within the app.
