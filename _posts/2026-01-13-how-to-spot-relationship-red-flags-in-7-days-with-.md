### **Step 2: The Neutral Observation Journal (Days 2-6)**

For the next five days, become a researcher of your own relationship. Each day, jot down 2-3 factual notes related to your core concerns. Avoid interpretations like "They were being rude." Instead, record the data: "Texted at 8 PM to cancel dinner planned for 7 PM, reason given was 'forgot.'" This creates an unbiased log for analysis.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid During Your Analysis

1.  **Confusing Incompatibility with Red Flags:** A red flag indicates harmful behavior (disrespect, manipulation). Simple differences in hobbies or introversion vs. extroversion are often just incompatibility. The AI helps differentiate by focusing on harmful behavioral cues.
2.  **Ignoring Context:** A single incident is rarely a definitive sign. The process's value is in spotting *patterns*. Input multiple data points into the scanner for an accurate read, not just one bad day.
3.  **Using the App as a Weapon:** This guide is for **self-reflection and clarity**, not to gather "ammunition" for a fight. The goal is personal insight to guide your actions, not to diagnose or attack another person.
4.  **Dismissing Your Gut When Data Aligns:** If your intuition screamed warning and the AI analysis consistently identifies problematic patterns, trust that synthesis. Don't explain away clear evidence due to fear of being alone.
