### Step 4: Fine-Tune with the Equalizer (Volume Booster - Sound Booster Feature)

Loudness isn't just about raw gain — it's about frequency balance. Tap the **Equalizer** tab. You'll see a 5-band EQ: 60Hz, 230Hz, 910Hz, 3.6kHz, and 14kHz.

**Common error:** Boosting all bands equally just makes everything louder and muddier. Adjust one band at a time and listen.

## Common Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

1. **Cranking boost to 200% immediately.** This causes clipping and can damage small speakers. Start at 130% and work up.
2. **Ignoring the equalizer.** Raw gain without EQ tuning sounds harsh. Always balance frequencies.
3. **Using bass boost with cheap earbuds.** The drivers can't handle it — you'll get rattle, not thump. Keep bass under 30% for budget gear.
4. **Forgetting to recalibrate when switching devices.** Each output has different tolerances. Re-run calibration when you plug in new headphones.
5. **Not disabling battery optimization.** The app will stop working in the background. Follow Step 8.
