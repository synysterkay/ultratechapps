## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid with AI Predictions

1.  **Treating AI as a Crystal Ball:** The biggest pitfall is assuming AI predictions are 100% certain. They are sophisticated probability estimates. A 75% chance still loses 25% of the time. Use them as a powerful guide, not an oracle.
2.  **Ignoring the "Why":** Don't just look at the predicted winner percentage. Always read the influencing factors. If the AI's key reason is "Home Team's star striker is doubtful," and news breaks that he's fit to play, the entire prediction may be invalid. AI is a starting point for your research.
3.  **Overcomplicating with Too Many Tools:** Beginners often try to use 5 different prediction apps and get conflicting signals, leading to analysis paralysis. Stick with one robust tool like Predictify: Soccer AI to build a consistent baseline, then layer on your own knowledge.
4.  **Chasing Losses with AI:** After a loss, the temptation is to immediately use the AI to find the next "sure thing" to recoup losses. This is emotional betting. The AI is a tool for strategic, planned decisions, not for impulsive reactions.
5.  **Neglecting Bankroll Management:** No prediction tool, no matter how advanced, negates the need for strict financial discipline. Never bet more than you can afford to lose, regardless of how high an AI's confidence percentage is.
