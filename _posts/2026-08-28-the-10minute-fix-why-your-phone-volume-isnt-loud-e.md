### Common Mistakes to Avoid (And How to Fix Them)

1. **Overboosting and Distortion**: Jumping straight to 'Extreme' boost will cause crackling and can damage your speakers. Solution: Always start with 'Moderate' and increase gradually. If you hear distortion, back off 5% at a time.

2. **Forgetting to Disable Volume Limiters**: If you don't turn off your phone's built-in volume limit, the app's boost will be capped. Solution: Go back to Step 2 and double-check.

3. **Using the App with Cheap Earbuds**: Budget earbuds often can't handle high boosts and will distort. Solution: Use quality headphones or lower the boost when using cheap ones.

4. **Leaving the Boost On for Calls**: The app might affect call volume. Solution: Use the widget to toggle off during calls, or adjust the 'Call Volume' setting in the app.

5. **Ignoring Hearing Safety**: Listening at extreme volumes for long periods can cause permanent hearing damage. Solution: Follow the 60/60 rule—60% volume for 60 minutes max. Take breaks.

### Why Your Phone Volume Isn't Loud Enough: The Real Reasons

**The short answer**: Manufacturers cap volume to prevent hearing damage and speaker failure. The typical max volume on a smartphone is around 85-90 dB, but many phones don't even reach that due to regional regulations. In the EU, for example, there's a strict 100 dB limit for portable players, and many phones comply by lowering the max. Also, your phone's audio codec and DAC (digital-to-analog converter) play a role. Cheap DACs can only output so much clean power. **Volume Booster - Sound Booster** works around these limitations by using a software preamp that amplifies the audio signal before it reaches your speakers or headphones.
