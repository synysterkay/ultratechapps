### 5 Common Mistakes That Ruin Your Speakers (And How to Avoid Them)

1.  **Maxing Out Every Slider:** Pushing all controls to 100% forces your speaker's tiny diaphragm beyond its physical limit, causing permanent damage through **clipping and distortion**. **Solution:** Use incremental gains. If you hear crackling, reduce the booster level immediately.
2.  **Ignoring Audio Source Quality:** Boosting a low-bitrate, compressed audio file will amplify the flaws and noise. **Solution:** Start with high-quality source files (e.g., 256kbps or higher) for the best results.
3.  **Forgetting to Disable When Using Headphones:** The intense amplification settings for speakers can be dangerously loud and damaging for headphones and your ears. **Solution:** Always disable the booster or switch to a headphone-specific preset before plugging in.
4.  **Leaving the App Running Unnecessarily:** Constant audio processing can slightly increase battery drain. **Solution:** Use the app's widget or quick-toggle to enable the booster only when you need it for speaker playback.
5.  **Expecting Studio-Quality Sound from Tiny Hardware:** This method enhances what your hardware can do; it doesn't replace a dedicated Bluetooth speaker. **Solution:** Have realistic expectations. You're gaining significant volume and clarity, not a concert hall experience.
