Before using the app, know what you're looking for. The five most common yet subtle red flags in modern dating are:
1.  **Love Bombing:** Excessive affection, grand promises, and rapid intensity designed to create dependency.
2.  **Future Faking:** Talking extensively about future plans (trips, moving in) with no concrete action, creating false intimacy.
3.  **Inconsistent Communication:** Hot-and-cold messaging, taking days to reply without explanation, keeping you off-balance.
4.  **Victim Mentality:** Portraying themselves as constantly wronged by exes or the world, avoiding personal accountability.
5.  **Boundary Testing:** Making small, "joking" criticisms or pushing minor limits to see how much you'll tolerate.

## 3 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Scanning for Red Flags

Even with a powerful tool, users can stumble. Avoid these pitfalls:

1.  **Over-Scanning Every Message:** Don't analyze every "good morning" text. Use the AI for specific, concerning patterns. Constant suspicion is itself a red flag. The tool is for clarity, not anxiety.
2.  **Ignoring Context:** The AI analyzes the text you provide. If you omit crucial context (e.g., they had a family emergency that caused inconsistent texting), the result will be skewed. Always be fair and factual in your input.
3.  **Dismissing Low-Risk Scores When Your Gut Screams:** The AI is a tool, not an oracle. If you have a strong visceral feeling of danger, trust it. Use a low-risk score as one data point, not a permission slip to ignore your intuition.
