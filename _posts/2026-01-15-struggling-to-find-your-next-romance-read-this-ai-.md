## Why Old-School Reading Apps and Recommendations Fail You

The traditional model is broken. Ebook platforms and subscription services operate on a one-to-many principle. They acquire rights to a finite catalog and push those same stories to millions of readers. Their recommendation engines are reactive, suggesting titles similar to what you've *already* read, trapping you in a feedback loop of the familiar. Want a story with a specific career for the lead, a particular setting, and a niche trope combination? Good luck.

Book clubs and social media recommendations help, but they're slow and rely on someone else's taste matching yours perfectly. You're left adapting your desires to fit the available content, rather than having content adapt to your desires. It's a passive, frustrating experience where you are always the consumer, never the co-creator of your own escape. The old solution was to 'just keep browsing.' The new solution is to change the game entirely.
