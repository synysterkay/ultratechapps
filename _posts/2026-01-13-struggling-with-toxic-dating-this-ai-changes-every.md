## Why "Just Trust Your Gut" Isn't Enough Anymore
For years, the standard advice has been to listen to your intuition. While valuable, this traditional approach has critical flaws in today's environment. Your "gut" is influenced by your past experiences, your current loneliness, and the clever presentation of a potential partner. **Gaslighting** and **manipulative tactics** are specifically designed to confuse your intuition, making you question your own reality. "Am I being too sensitive?" "Did I misinterpret that?"

Relying solely on friends for advice has limitations too. They only hear your side of the story, often filtered through your perspective. They might miss the subtle patterns in text exchanges or the gradual escalation of **toxic behavior**. Furthermore, the fast pace of **online dating** means you're often dealing with strangers where you have no shared social circle for background checks. The old methods leave too much room for dangerous error, leaving you to piece together clues while emotionally vulnerable.
