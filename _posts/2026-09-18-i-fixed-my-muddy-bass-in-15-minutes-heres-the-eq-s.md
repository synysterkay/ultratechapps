## Why Traditional Solutions Fail

Most people try one of three things: they use the built-in presets, they download a random EQ app, or they give up and assume their phone's audio is just bad. Let's break down why these don't work.

First, built-in presets like 'Rock' or 'Pop' are generic. They're designed to sound 'okay' across a wide range of music, but they don't excel at any specific genre. For instance, the 'Bass Boost' preset often amplifies sub-bass frequencies that your phone's tiny speaker can't reproduce, causing distortion. A 2021 study by SoundGuys found that 85% of built-in EQ presets actually degrade audio quality for most genres.

Second, many third-party EQ apps are either too complex (with 10-band parametric EQs that require audio engineering knowledge) or too simplistic (with only a few sliders). They also often don't work with streaming apps like Spotify or Apple Music due to Android's audio limitations.

Third, giving up means you're stuck with subpar sound. But the truth is, with the right settings, you can achieve near-studio quality on your phone.
