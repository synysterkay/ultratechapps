## Common Mistakes to Avoid with AI Date Planning

While AI is powerful, avoiding these pitfalls will ensure your dates remain authentic and connected.

1.  **Over-Reliance on AI:** The AI is a suggestion engine, not a replacement for your intuition. Always customize its ideas with personal touches that you know your partner will love.
2.  **Skipping Collaboration:** The biggest advantage over traditional planning is real-time collaboration. If you plan in a vacuum and just present a *fait accompli*, you miss the bonding aspect of planning together and risk mismatch in expectations.
3.  **Being Too Vague:** When prompting the AI, generic inputs like "fun date" yield generic results. Be specific with your criteria (budget, cuisine, activity type) to get truly tailored **personalized date ideas**.
4.  **Forgetting the Logistics:** An AI can suggest a romantic picnic, but it's on you to check the weather, book a spot, or pack the blanket. Use the app's plan-building step to add these crucial logistical notes.
