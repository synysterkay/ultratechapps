## Common Mistakes

1. **Maxing out boost immediately** – Start at 50% and increase gradually. Sudden high volume can blow speakers.
2. **Ignoring distortion** – If it crackles, back off. Clean sound at 80% beats distorted at 100%.
3. **Using bass boost with small speakers** – Tiny phone speakers can't handle heavy bass; use it sparingly.
4. **Forgetting to turn off the app** – Leaving it on can drain battery and cause unintended loudness. Use the quick toggle.
5. **Not updating the app** – Updates include performance improvements and new features. Keep it current.
