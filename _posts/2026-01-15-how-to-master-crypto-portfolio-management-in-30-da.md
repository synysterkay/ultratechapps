### Step 2: Define Your Investment Goals & Risk Tolerance
Before tracking metrics, know your destination. Are you a long-term HODLer, an active swing trader, or something in between? Your goals dictate which metrics matter most. Spend five minutes in the app's settings or your own notes to jot down your primary objective (e.g., "20% annual growth") and your maximum acceptable drawdown (e.g., "No more than 15% loss in a week"). This context will make the AI's insights profoundly more relevant.

### Step 3: Track Metric #1 - Portfolio Allocation & Diversification
This is your defense against catastrophic loss. Navigate to the "Portfolio" or "Allocation" tab in Crypto AI: Trading Analyzer. The AI will visually break down your holdings by asset (Bitcoin, Ethereum, altcoins) and category (Large Cap, DeFi, NFT). **The secret most people miss:** True diversification isn't just owning many coins; it's owning uncorrelated assets. The app's correlation matrix feature shows how your assets move in relation to each other. What you'll see: A pie chart or bar graph showing if 70% of your wealth is in one volatile altcoin—a major red flag. Aim for balance.

### Step 4: Track Metric #2 - Overall Profit & Loss (P&L) Performance
Are you actually making money? Go to the "Performance" or "Analytics" section. Crypto AI: Trading Analyzer calculates your total realized and unrealized P&L, both in fiat value and percentage terms. It tracks this daily, weekly, and monthly. This metric cuts through the noise and tells you the bottom line. What you'll see: A clear chart showing your portfolio's growth trajectory. ### Step 5: Track Metric #3 - Risk Exposure (Beta & Volatility)
High returns mean nothing without understanding the risk taken to get them. Within the app's advanced analytics, look for "Risk Metrics" or "Volatility Analysis." Key here is **Portfolio Beta**—a measure of how much your portfolio moves compared to the overall crypto market (often benchmarked to Bitcoin). A beta above 1 means you're taking on more risk than the market. What you'll see: A numerical beta score and a volatility chart. The AI helps you identify which specific assets are driving your risk up, allowing for targeted adjustments.

Don't just track coins, track narratives. The crypto market moves in cycles driven by sectors like DeFi, Gaming, or Layer 1s. ### Step 7: Track Metric #5 - Cost Basis & Holding Period
Tax efficiency and strategy validation hinge on this. The app automatically calculates the average purchase price (cost basis) for each of your holdings and the average length of time you've held them. This reveals if your winning trades are from quick flips or patient holding. What you'll see: For each asset, a clear display of your average buy price versus current price, and a "Time Held" metric. Long holding periods on losing assets may indicate a need to re-evaluate your thesis.

## 5 Common Crypto Portfolio Management Mistakes to Avoid
1.  **Ignoring Diversification:** Putting all your capital into one or two assets is gambling, not investing. Use Crypto AI: Trading Analyzer's allocation chart weekly to check for over-concentration.
2.  **Chasing Past Performance:** Buying an asset simply because it's "up 100% this week" is a classic trap. The AI's trend analysis helps you understand if a trend is sustainable or overextended.
3.  **Not Accounting for Fees & Slippage:** Small trade fees erode returns dramatically. The app's performance analytics use your actual trade data, giving you a net return figure that includes these hidden costs.
4.  **Emotional Rebalancing:** Selling everything during a dip or FOMO-buying at a peak. Let the metrics guide you. Set rules (e.g., "Rebalance if any asset exceeds 25% of portfolio") and use the app's alerts to notify you when thresholds are hit.
5.  **Neglecting Macro Trends:** Being overly focused on your specific coins while missing sector-wide downturns. Regularly review the sector performance metric to ensure your portfolio aligns with broader market health.
