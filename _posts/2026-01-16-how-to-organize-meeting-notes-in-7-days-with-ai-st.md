Start by gathering all your recent meeting notes from various apps, notebooks, and documents. Don't organize them yet—just collect. Look for common pain points: Are action items buried? Are key decisions hard to find? Is there no consistent format? This audit reveals your specific organizational gaps. Your goal is to understand the "before" picture so you can appreciate the "after."

### Day 2: Define Your New Note-Taking Structure

Today, you'll design a simple template. Every note you take should have four consistent sections: **Key Decisions**, **Action Items (with owners)**, **Main Discussion Points**, and **Follow-Up Questions**. This structure forces clarity during the meeting itself. You can jot notes freely, but mentally categorize them into these buckets. This prepares your raw material for AI processing.

### Day 3: Capture & Process with Smart Notes

This is where the magic begins. During your next meeting, use **Smart Notes - AI Meeting Summary** to record the audio. After the meeting, open the app. You'll see the audio file ready for processing. Tap the AI summary button. The app will transcribe the conversation and analyze it using natural language processing.

### Day 4: Review & Tag the AI Summary

Open **Smart Notes** to see your analyzed meeting. **What you'll see:** A clean, structured summary split into logical sections like "Meeting Summary," "Action Items," "Key Points," and "Questions." The AI has already done the heavy lifting of extracting tasks and decisions from the raw transcript. Now, add tags. For example, tag an action item with `#client-xyz` and `#urgent`. Tag a key decision with `#q2-strategy`. This tagging is the cornerstone of searchability.

### Day 5: Export & Integrate into Your Systems

Don't let insights stay trapped in one app. Use **Smart Notes**' export features. **What you'll do:** Export the formatted summary as a text file to your designated cloud drive (e.g., `/Work/Meetings/2024-05-ProjectX`). More crucially, copy the clear action items and paste them directly into your task manager, preserving the owner and deadline identified by the AI. This creates a seamless bridge between meeting discussion and personal execution.

### Day 6: Build Your Searchable Knowledge Base

Your cloud drive folder is now becoming a knowledge base. Ensure every exported summary from **Smart Notes** follows a consistent naming convention: `YYYY-MM-DD_MeetingTopic_Summary.txt`. Because you tagged items within Smart Notes and used clear filenames, you can now use your drive's search function to find everything related to `#client-xyz` or "budget discussion" across all meetings in seconds.

### Day 7: The Weekly Review & Refinement

Set a 15-minute weekly recurring appointment. In this time, open **Smart Notes** and review all meetings from the past week. Check off completed action items in the app. Look for patterns in the summaries. Ask yourself: Is the tagging system working? Do any action items need escalation? This weekly ritual ensures your system stays alive and adapts to your needs.

## 3 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Skipping the Tagging Step:** The AI gives you a perfect summary, but without tags, it's just another document. Tags are the hyperlinks of your knowledge base. Spending 60 seconds adding 3-4 tags per meeting saves hours of searching later.
2.  **Keeping Notes in Siloes:** The biggest pitfall is not integrating your action items with your main task manager. If your to-dos from meetings live separately, they will be forgotten. The export and copy-paste step is non-negotiable for accountability.
3.  **Treating the Summary as Final:** The AI summary is a fantastic first draft, but it's not infallible. Always skim it for context. Sometimes a crucial nuance might be missed. A quick human review ensures accuracy and reinforces the information in your memory.
