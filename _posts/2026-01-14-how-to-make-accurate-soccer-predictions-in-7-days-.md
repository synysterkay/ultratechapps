## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Treating AI as a Crystal Ball:** The biggest pitfall is assuming AI predictions are 100% certain. They are sophisticated probability estimates, not guarantees. A team with an 80% win probability still loses 20% of the time. Use the percentages to assess risk, not to seek certainty.
2.  **Ignoring Your Own Knowledge:** Blindly following AI without adding your own soccer IQ is a mistake. The AI might not factor in a last-minute manager sacking or torrential rain affecting a team's style of play. Always layer the data with current news and situational context.
3.  **Chasing Losses with AI:** If a prediction is wrong, don't immediately use the app to find a "sure thing" to recoup losses. This leads to emotional, not analytical, decisions. Stick to your process and league focus.
4.  **Overlooking the "Draw" Probability:** Many analysts focus only on win/loss. A high "Draw" probability (e.g., 35%+) is a significant AI signal, often indicating evenly matched teams or tactical styles that cancel each other out. It's a valuable insight for certain betting markets.
