### My Testing Methodology and Criteria

I measured decibel output with a calibrated SPL meter, checked distortion using a spectrum analyzer app, and gathered feedback from 5 listeners. Each app was tested for 7 days in real-world scenarios: commuting, gym, and quiet home listening. My criteria included volume increase, sound quality preservation, ease of use, and preset variety.
