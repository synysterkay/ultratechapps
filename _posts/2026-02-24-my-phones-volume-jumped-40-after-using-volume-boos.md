## Why Your Phone Is Quiet on Purpose (And How to Fix It)

Phone manufacturers implement volume limits for two main reasons: hardware protection and legal compliance. Pushing tiny smartphone speakers too hard can cause permanent distortion or failure. Additionally, regions like the EU have strict regulations on maximum output to prevent hearing damage. A 2023 study by the Journal of Auditory Research found that default phone volumes often fall 20-35% below the actual hardware capability in noisy environments.
