## Why I Ran This Comparison

Ever been at a party where your phone just can't cut through the noise? You crank it to max, but everyone's still asking "Can you turn it up?" That frustration drove me to test the real-world performance of volume booster apps versus external speakers.

I spent 3 weekends hosting 5 small gatherings (10-15 people each) in different settings: a living room, a backyard, a small park, a garage, and a bedroom. I measured sound levels with a calibrated dB meter at 3 feet distance. My goal? Answer one question: **volume booster vs. external speakers: which is better for parties?**

## Testing Methodology and Criteria

I tested 7 volume booster apps (including Volume Booster - Sound Booster) and 3 external speaker types (Bluetooth portable, wired bookshelf, and a mini party speaker). Each was tested with the same 3 songs: a bass-heavy track, a vocal-focused acoustic piece, and a classic rock anthem. I measured peak dB, sustained dB, distortion (subjective rating 1-10), and battery drain.

**Criteria weights:**
1. **Peak Volume Output (30%)** – How loud can it get?
2. **Sound Quality (25%)** – No one likes distorted audio
3. **Portability (20%)** – Can you bring it anywhere?
4. **Battery Impact (15%)** – Does it kill your phone?
5. **Ease of Use (10%)** – How fast can you set it up?
