## What Actually Happens When You Boost Volume in Software

Here's the part most articles skip. A phone speaker has a fixed physical driver — usually 11–13mm in modern smartphones. That driver can only push so much air. When you max out the system volume, you've hit the hardware ceiling.

A **sound amplifier app** like Volume Booster - Sound Booster works differently. It applies gain amplification to the audio signal *before* it reaches the driver, then uses compression and equalization to keep the output from clipping into distortion. Think of it like turning up the pressure in a garden hose: the hose stays the same size, but more water comes through it per second.

The catch? If you push gain too hard, you get crackle. That's why the app's built-in limiter matters — it caps the boost before distortion becomes audible.
