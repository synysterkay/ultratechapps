## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Writing with AI

1.  **Being Too Vague:** Prompting the AI with "write a romance scene" will yield generic results. Always provide specific details about character emotions, setting, and action.
2.  **Accepting the First Output:** The first generation is a starting point, not the final product. The real skill is in the iterative refinement—asking the AI to expand, tweak, or rephrase.
3.  **Losing Your Authorial Voice:** It's easy to let the AI's style dominate. Regularly inject sentences, descriptions, and turns of phrase that sound like *you* to maintain a unique voice.
4.  **Neglecting Plot Consistency:** AI generates text in chunks. You must be the keeper of the overall plot. Keep notes on key events and character details to ensure everything aligns from chapter to chapter.
5.  **Forgetting the Emotional Core:** Romance is about feeling. Don't get so caught up in generating words that you forget to check if each scene advances the emotional relationship. Every interaction should build tension, intimacy, or conflict.
