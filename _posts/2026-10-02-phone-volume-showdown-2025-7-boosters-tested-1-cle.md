**Rating: 7.8/10**

Focused on low frequencies, this app boosted bass by 12 dB but only increased overall volume by 10 dB. It's ideal for hip-hop and EDM fans, but not for general use. Ads appear every 3 songs, which is annoying. Battery impact: 5% per hour.

### 4. Super Loud Volume Booster

**Rating: 7.0/10**

Despite its name, it delivered only a 15 dB boost. Sound became distorted at maximum levels. The interface is cluttered with ads. However, it's free and works on older Android devices. Battery drain: 7% per hour.

### 5. Volume+ (Android)

**Rating: 6.5/10**

This app boosted volume by 12 dB but caused significant distortion. It also requires root access for full functionality, limiting its audience. Not recommended for casual users.

### 6. Boom: Music Player

**Rating: 6.0/10**

Boom is more of a music player with a 3D sound effect. It boosted volume by 14 dB but altered the audio signature noticeably. The interface is beautiful, but it's not a dedicated booster. Battery drain: 8% per hour.

### 7. Equalizer FX

**Rating: 5.5/10**

This app provides EQ but only a 10 dB volume boost. It's dated and buggy on newer Android versions. Not recommended.
