### 2. Volume Booster GOODEV

**Loudness Boost:** +8 dB (78 dB to 86 dB).
**Distortion:** Noticeable at 80%+ boost.

**Setup Time:** 10 seconds.

GOODEV is a popular free option, but it's basic. The boost is decent, but I heard crackling on bass-heavy tracks. It also lacks an EQ, so you can't fine-tune. For casual use, it's fine, but audiophiles will want more.

### 3. Bass Booster & Equalizer

**Loudness Boost:** +6 dB (78 dB to 84 dB).
**Distortion:** Moderate at high volumes.
**Battery Drain:** 7% after 30 minutes.
**Setup Time:** 30 seconds.

This app focuses on EQ rather than raw volume. The bass boost is strong, but the overall loudness increase is modest. It's great for headphones but not for speakerphone calls.

### 4. Speaker Boost

**Loudness Boost:** +5 dB (78 dB to 83 dB).
**Distortion:** High at max boost.
**Battery Drain:** 4% after 30 minutes.
**Setup Time:** 20 seconds.

Speaker Boost is simple but risky. At maximum, I heard distortion that made vocals sound robotic. It's okay for podcasts, but not music.

### 5. Volume+ (Android only)

**Loudness Boost:** +7 dB (78 dB to 85 dB).
**Distortion:** Low at moderate boost, high at max.
**Battery Drain:** 6% after 30 minutes.
**Setup Time:** 25 seconds.

Volume+ offers granular control but requires root access for full features. Without root, it's limited. The UI feels dated.

### 6. EQ Booster

**Loudness Boost:** +4 dB (78 dB to 82 dB).
**Distortion:** Low, but boost is weak.
**Battery Drain:** 2% after 30 minutes.
**Setup Time:** 15 seconds.

EQ Booster is more of an equalizer than a volume booster. It's gentle and safe, but if you need serious loudness, look elsewhere.
