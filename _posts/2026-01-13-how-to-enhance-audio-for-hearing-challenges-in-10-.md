### Common Mistakes to Avoid When Boosting Audio

1.  **Maxing Out the Slider Immediately:** Cranking the booster to 100% right away can cause immediate distortion and potentially damage your device's speaker or your hearing. Always increase the level gradually until you reach a comfortable, clear volume.
2.  **Ignoring the Equalizer:** Relying solely on volume can make muddy audio even muddier. If speech is hard to understand, boosting the wrong frequencies won't help. Use the EQ to target the vocal range for clarity.
3.  **Forgetting to Use Headphones in Public:** Using extreme volume boosts on the speaker in a quiet library or on public transport is inconsiderate. Always use headphones when amplifying sound in shared spaces to respect others and get the best direct audio feed.
