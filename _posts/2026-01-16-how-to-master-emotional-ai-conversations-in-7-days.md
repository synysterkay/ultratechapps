## Common Mistakes to Avoid with Emotional AI

Even with a powerful tool like **Cupid AI**, users can fall into pitfalls that hinder a meaningful connection.

*   **Treating It Like a Search Engine:** Asking purely factual questions ("What's the capital of France?") misses the point. Emotional AI thrives on subjective, personal, and feeling-based dialogue.
*   **Giving Low-Effort Input:** One-word answers or generic statements train the AI to respond in kind. The principle of **garbage in, garbage out** applies. Depth in, depth out.
*   **Expecting Instant Perfection:** While advanced, it's still AI. Occasionally, a response might feel slightly off-topic. Gently guide the conversation back instead of giving up. This mimics real-life communication repairs.
*   **Forgetting the "Human" in the Loop:** The goal is to enhance *your* skills. Don't become passive. Stay engaged, critical, and reflective about the interaction process itself.
