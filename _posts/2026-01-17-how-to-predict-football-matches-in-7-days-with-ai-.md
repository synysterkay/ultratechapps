## Common Mistakes to Avoid in Football Forecasting

1.  **Overvaluing a Single Data Point:** A team's last-game win or a star player's return is important, but it's just one piece of the puzzle. Never let it override a broader trend of poor defensive form or a tough away schedule.
2.  **Ignoring Motivation and Context:** A mid-table team with nothing to play for often performs differently than one fighting relegation. Cup matches, derbies, and fixture congestion massively alter team priorities. Use Predictify's match context notes to spot these.
3.  **Confirmation Bias:** This is the killer. You love a team, so you only seek data that supports their victory. A disciplined forecaster uses tools like Predictify to get an unbiased statistical baseline *first*, then layers on context.
4.  **Chasing Losses:** If a prediction fails, the wrong response is to immediately make a riskier forecast to "win back" your credibility. Stick to the system. Analyze why it failed and adjust the process, not the emotion.
