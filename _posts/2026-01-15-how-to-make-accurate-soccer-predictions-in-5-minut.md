## Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using AI Predictions

1.  **Blindly Following the AI:** The AI is a powerful tool, not an oracle. A 95% probability can still be wrong. Always use it as your primary analyst, but consider last-minute team news the AI might not have captured.
2.  **Ignoring the "Why":** Don't just look at the final percentage. The real value is in the advanced analytics and insights. If you skip Step 5 and 6, you're missing the educational component that makes you smarter long-term.
3.  **Chasing Losses with AI:** If a prediction was wrong, avoid the temptation to immediately use the app to find a "sure thing" to recoup losses. This is emotional betting. Stick to your system and use the AI for planned analysis, not impulsive reactions.
4.  **Overlooking Small Leagues:** AI models can be exceptionally sharp for major leagues with vast data. Be slightly more cautious with predictions for lower-division or obscure leagues where data quality might be thinner.
