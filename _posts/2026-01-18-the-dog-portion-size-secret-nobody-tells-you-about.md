### How to Calculate the Right Portion Size for Your Dog's Breed

This is the core of the secret. While the classic formula is a start, it's just the foundation. Here’s a direct answer to how it's done:

**Featured Snippet Answer:** To calculate your dog's daily calorie needs, first determine their Resting Energy Requirement (RER): 70 x (body weight in kg)^0.75. Then, multiply the RER by a factor based on their life stage and activity level (e.g., 1.6 for neutered adult weight loss). Finally, divide the total calories by the kcal/cup of your specific food.
