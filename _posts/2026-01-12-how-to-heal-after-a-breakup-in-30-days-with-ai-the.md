### Your 30-Day Healing Roadmap: A Step-by-Step Tutorial

**Step 1: Acknowledge the Ending Completely**
The first step in any healing process is radical acceptance. Stop minimizing the impact or telling yourself you "should" be over it. Spend 10 minutes writing down the full truth of the relationship's end. This isn't about blame, but about acknowledging the reality of the loss. This act of validation is a powerful catalyst for emotional processing and moving forward.

**Step 2: Identify Your 5 Readiness Signals**
Healing begins when you're ready. Look for these five signs:
1.  **The Anger Subsides:** The sharp, reactive anger has dulled into a more reflective sadness.
2.  **Nostalgia Overwhelms Obsession:** You think of happy memories more than agonizing over "what ifs."
3.  **You Seek Solutions, Not Just Sympathy:** You're looking for "how to get over a breakup" rather than just venting.
4.  **Curiosity About the Future Returns:** You have fleeting moments of wondering what's next for *you*.
5.  **You Can See Their Flaws Clearly:** The idealized pedestal has crumbled, allowing for a more balanced view.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid on Your Healing Journey

1.  **Rushing the Process:** Emotional healing has its own timeline. Trying to force yourself to "be over it" by a certain date often backfires, leading to repressed emotions that resurface later. Allow yourself to feel the waves of grief as they come.
2.  **Isolating Yourself:** While reflection is crucial, complete social withdrawal can deepen depression. Balance your solo app work with light, supportive social contact, even if it's just a short call with a friend.
3.  **Stalking Your Ex Online:** This is the number one behavior that resets your healing clock to zero. It fuels obsession and false narratives. Use app features like the "Urge Surfing" tool to manage the impulse to check their social media.
