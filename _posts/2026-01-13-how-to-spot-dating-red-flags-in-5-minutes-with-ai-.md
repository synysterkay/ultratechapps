Before using any tool, you must know what you're looking for. The five most common red flags in modern dating are:
1.  **Love Bombing:** Overwhelming affection and future-faking early on to create intense attachment.
2.  **Inconsistent Communication:** Hot-and-cold behavior that keeps you guessing and anxious.
3.  **Disrespectful Language:** Put-downs disguised as jokes, ignoring your boundaries, or demeaning comments.
4.  **Victim Mentality:** Constantly blaming others and refusing to take accountability for their life.
5.  **Financial Irresponsibility/Dependency:** Early requests for money or a pattern of reckless spending.

## 3 Common Mistakes to Avoid

Even with a powerful tool, users can stumble. Avoid these pitfalls:

1.  **Dismissing 'Minor' Flags:** The most dangerous mistake is rationalizing small concerns away. A demeaning "joke" or a broken promise are foundational bricks in a wall of disrespect. Use the app to log *everything* that feels off—let the AI help you see the cumulative pattern.
2.  **Over-Analyzing Every Word:** Don't use the scanner to pathologize normal, awkward human interaction. Use it for genuine concerns, not for analyzing a simple "hey" text. The tool is for substantive conversations and repeated behaviors.
3.  **Ignoring Your Gut After a 'Clean' Scan:** AI is a brilliant assistant, not an omniscient oracle. If the app finds no major flags but your intuition is screaming, trust yourself. The AI may lack context only you possess. Use it as a second opinion, not a final verdict.
