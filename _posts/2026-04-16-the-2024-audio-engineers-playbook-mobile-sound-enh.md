## 5 Common Audio Enhancement Mistakes (And How to Fix Them)

1.  **Maxing Out Every Slider:** Cranking all controls to 100% causes severe distortion and can damage speakers. **Fix:** Use the calibration method in Step 3. More is not always better.
2.  **Ignoring the Source Quality:** Enhancing a low-bitrate MP3 will only amplify its flaws. **Fix:** Use high-quality source files (e.g., 256kbps AAC or higher) for the best results.
3.  **Creating Extreme EQ "V" or "U" Shapes:** Drastically cutting mids creates a hollow, scooped sound. **Fix:** Aim for gentle, broad curves. Boosting bass and treble slightly while keeping mids neutral is often more musical.
4.  **Forgetting to Create App-Specific Profiles:** Using a heavy bass profile for a podcast will muddy speech. **Fix:** Use the app's profile feature to save tailored settings for different use cases.
5.  **Enabling All Effects Simultaneously Without Listening:** Stacking bass boost, a heavy EQ, and the virtualizer can create a chaotic, unnatural soundscape. **Fix:** Enable one effect at a time, listen, then add another. Build your sound layer by layer.
