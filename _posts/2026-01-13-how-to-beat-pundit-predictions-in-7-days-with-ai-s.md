## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Comparing Predictions
1.  **Confusing Correlation with Causation:** Just because a pundit mentions a stat and gets the prediction right doesn't mean that stat was the cause. The AI might have weighted ten other more important factors.
2.  **Ignoring the Probability:** AI tools like Predictify give a percentage chance (e.g., 65% home win). Don't treat a 65% prediction as a "wrong" call if the 35% outcome happens; understand it was the less likely event.
3.  **Overfitting to Recent Events:** Both humans and algorithms can be swayed by the last game. A pundit might overreact to a shocking loss, while a good AI balances recent form with longer-term trends.
4.  **Neglecting the "Unknown Unknowns":** No model can account for a sudden red card or a moment of individual brilliance. Use AI to understand the likely baseline, not to guarantee an exact outcome.
5.  **Becoming a Slave to the Output:** The goal is to enhance your judgment, not replace it. If your knowledge of a team's morale or a hidden injury contradicts a clean data set, factor it in. You are the final analyst.
