## Why Your Current Note-Taking System is Failing You
You've tried the traditional solutions. Maybe you rotate the "note-taker" role, sacrificing one person's full participation. Perhaps you rely on recording every meeting, creating a digital graveyard of files no one has time to review. Or, everyone scribbles their own version, leading to conflicting narratives and the dreaded "he said, she said."

These methods fail with empathy. They assume someone has the bandwidth to be a perfect, unbiased stenographer while also contributing meaningfully. They create a single point of failure. If the designated note-taker zones out for a moment, that insight is gone forever. Recording meetings just shifts the burden; now you have to listen to the whole thing *again* to find the five important minutes. It's a band-aid on a bullet wound, adding more work instead of removing it. You're solving the symptom (needing a record) but not the disease (wasting human potential on administrative tasks).
