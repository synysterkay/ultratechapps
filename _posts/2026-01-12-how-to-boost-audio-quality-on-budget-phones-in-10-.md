### Your Step-by-Step Guide to Better Phone Audio
Follow these steps in order to build upon each improvement for the best results.

#### Step 1: Clean Your Phone's Speakers
It sounds simple, but dust and lint clogging the speaker grille is a major cause of muffled sound. Gently use a soft-bristled brush, like a clean toothbrush, or a blast of compressed air to clear the grilles. You'll immediately notice clearer highs and less vibration distortion.

#### Step 2: Disable Absolute Volume in Bluetooth Settings
If you use wireless headphones, this is a game-changer. Go to your phone's **Settings > Developer Options** (you may need to enable this by tapping 'Build Number' 7 times in 'About Phone'). Find 'Disable Absolute Volume' and toggle it ON. This gives your audio apps and **Volume Booster - Sound Booster** more granular control over Bluetooth volume levels, preventing sudden, jarring volume jumps.
