## Comparison Criteria: What I Measured and Why

I weighted each factor based on real-world importance for workouts and commutes:

1. **Maximum Volume Boost (25% weight)** - Measured in dB increase from stock max volume using a calibrated sound meter app.
2. **Audio Clarity (25% weight)** - Subjective rating from 1-10 on distortion, static, and bass presence at max boost.
3. **Battery Drain (15% weight)** - Percentage of battery used during a 45-minute session with the app active.
4. **Ease of Use (15% weight)** - Time to open app, apply settings, and start listening (measured in seconds).
5. **EQ Presets & Customization (10% weight)** - Number of presets and ability to fine-tune frequencies.
6. **Background Stability (10% weight)** - Whether the app crashed or reset volume during use.
