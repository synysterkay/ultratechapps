### **Step-by-Step: Spotting Red Flags with Red Flag Scanner AI**
Follow these steps to transform from confused to confident in your dating assessments.

**Step 1: Know Your Top 5 Red Flags**
Before using any tool, know what you're looking for. The five most common modern dating red flags are:
1.  **Love Bombing:** Excessive flattery, rapid declarations of strong feelings, and overwhelming attention designed to create instant intimacy and dependency.
2.  **Inconsistent Communication:** Hot-and-cold behavior, taking hours or days to reply without explanation, and creating anxiety through unpredictability.
3.  **Future Faking:** Making grand promises about the future (trips, meeting family) very early on, with no actionable steps, to create false hope.
4.  **Negging and Passive-Aggression:** Backhanded compliments or subtle insults disguised as jokes, meant to undermine your confidence.
5.  **Avoidance of Accountability:** Never apologizing sincerely, deflecting blame ("You're too sensitive"), or using "I'm just bad at texting" as a permanent excuse.

*   **Over-Analyzing Every Single Message:** Don't fall into the paralysis-by-analysis trap. Use the app for concerning patterns or confusing interactions, not to dissect every "hey." This leads to unnecessary anxiety.
