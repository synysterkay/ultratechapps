## Common Mistakes to Avoid with AI Note-Taking

1.  **Poor Audio Quality:** The AI's accuracy depends on clear audio. Avoid recording in noisy cafes or with the device too far from speakers. For virtual calls, record the direct audio stream if possible, or use a quiet room.
2.  **Skipping the Human Review:** Never share the raw AI output without a quick scan. While highly accurate, it may misinterpret homophones or niche jargon. A 2-minute review prevents misunderstandings.
3.  **Not Informing Participants:** Always get consent before recording a meeting. Transparency is ethical and often a legal requirement. A simple "I'll be recording for accurate notes" at the start suffices.
4.  **Letting Notes Pile Up:** The system breaks if you don't process summaries promptly. Review, tag, and share notes right after the meeting while context is fresh. This habit ensures your **knowledge management** stays current.
5.  **Ignoring Organization Features:** Using the app only for transcription wastes its potential. Use tags, folders, and the search function to build a retrievable knowledge base. This turns your notes from a archive into an active asset.
