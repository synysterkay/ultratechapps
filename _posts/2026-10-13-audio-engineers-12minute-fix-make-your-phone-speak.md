## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Boosting volume without cleaning first.** Dust muffles sound; boosting amplifies distortion. Always clean first.
2. **Using max boost on all audio.** Different content needs different settings. Music can handle more bass; podcasts need clarity.
3. **Ignoring distortion.** If you hear crackling, lower the boost. Long-term distortion damages speakers.
4. **Not updating the app.** Updates often include performance improvements and bug fixes.
5. **Overlooking phone case interference.** Some cases block speakers. Remove the case when testing.
