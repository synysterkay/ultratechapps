## Why We Miss Red Flags: The Psychology of Love and Bias

Our brains are wired for connection, not cold analysis. When we feel attraction or begin to develop feelings, powerful psychological mechanisms click into gear. **Confirmation bias** leads us to seek out evidence that supports our hopeful narrative and ignore contradictory signals. The **halo effect** makes us overlook flaws because we're captivated by a single great trait. **Emotional investment** makes walking away harder the more time we've spent. These aren't character flaws; they're human nature. The key to smarter dating isn't to become emotionless, but to supplement your intuition with an objective, insightful tool.
