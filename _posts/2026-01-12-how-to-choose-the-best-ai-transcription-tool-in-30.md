## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid When Choosing

1.  **Prioritizing Price Over Value:** The cheapest tool may cost you more in correction time. Calculate value as time saved versus monthly cost.
2.  **Ignoring the Summary Feature:** Thousands have discovered that transcription is only half the battle. A tool that doesn't summarize forces you to re-read everything, negating the time saved.
3.  **Not Testing with Your Own Voice/Content:** Accents, background noise, and industry slang vary. A tool that works for a tech reviewer might fail in your boardroom.
4.  **Overlooking Mobile Usability:** If you take calls on the go or record in-person chats, a robust mobile app like Smart Notes is essential. Don't be limited to desktop-only solutions.
5.  **Forgetting About Security:** If you handle sensitive client or company information, ensure the tool has clear data privacy and encryption policies. Don't risk a data leak for convenience.
