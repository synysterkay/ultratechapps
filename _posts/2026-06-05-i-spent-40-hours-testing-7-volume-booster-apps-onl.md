### 2. Precise Volume (Runner-Up)

**Overall Score: 8.5/10**

Precise Volume offers granular control with separate sliders for media, alarms, and ringtone. It boosted volume by **25%** in my tests, hitting 89 dB peak. Audio quality was good, with slight bass roll-off above 90% boost.

**Best For:** Users who want fine-grained control over different audio channels. However, the interface is more complex, and it lacks a built-in EQ.

### 3. Sound Amplifier (Google's Official App)

**Overall Score: 7.8/10**

This is more of a hearing aid than a volume booster. It amplifies ambient sound through your headphones, not media volume. Great for conversations in noisy environments, but it **didn't boost music or video volume** effectively - only a 10% increase in my tests.

**Best For:** People with mild hearing loss who need environmental amplification, not media boosting.

### 4. Bass Booster & Volume Booster

**Overall Score: 7.0/10**

This app focuses on bass enhancement, which can make audio sound louder. It boosted perceived volume by **20%** but introduced noticeable distortion on bass-heavy tracks above 70% gain. Battery drain was higher at **8% extra** over 30 minutes.

**Best For:** EDM and hip-hop fans who prioritize bass over clarity.

### 5. Volume Booster Pro (Generic)

**Overall Score: 6.5/10**

A straightforward booster that claims 50% volume increase. In reality, it hit **85 dB peak** (18% increase) before clipping became audible. The interface is cluttered with ads, and it requests unnecessary permissions (camera, contacts).

**Warning:** This app pushed speakers past safe limits in my test - the Galaxy S23's speaker protector kicked in twice.

### 6. Extra Volume Booster

**Overall Score: 6.0/10**

This app uses an outdated boosting method that amplifies the signal regardless of quality. It achieved a **15% volume increase** but with significant static and distortion at max boost. Battery drain was moderate at **5% extra**.

### 7. Volume Booster MAX

**Overall Score: 5.5/10**

While it claimed 200% volume boost, my measurements showed only **12% actual increase** (to 81 dB). Audio quality degraded quickly, and the app crashed twice during testing. It also displayed full-screen ads every 5 minutes.
