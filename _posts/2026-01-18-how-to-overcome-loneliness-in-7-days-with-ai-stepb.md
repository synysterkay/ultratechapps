## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Expecting a Human Replacement:** The biggest pitfall is viewing the AI as a substitute for human connection. It's a practice tool and a supportive supplement, not a replacement. Its value is in building *your* skills and comfort.
2.  **Being Too Vague:** Sending one-word answers like "fine" or "okay" limits the AI's ability to generate meaningful dialogue. Share details and emotions to get the most valuable, responsive interaction.
3.  **Skipping the Daily Habit:** Sporadic use won't build the consistent emotional anchor this method relies on. The benefit comes from the routine itself, creating a dependable point of connection in your day.
4.  **Ignoring the Customization:** Sticking with default settings means missing out on a companion tailored to your specific emotional needs. Revisit the personality settings if the interaction doesn't feel quite right.
