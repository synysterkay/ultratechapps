## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid

1.  **Over-Reliance on the Score:** Treat the Relationship Health Score as a starting point for investigation, not a final verdict. A medium score with one critical red flag (like disrespect) is more important than a high score with several minor quirks.
2.  **Scanning Out of Context:** AI is powerful, but it needs context. Avoid scanning very short, joke-filled initial greetings. The best analysis comes from substantive conversations about plans, values, or past experiences.
3.  **Ignoring Your Intuition:** If your gut screams "no" but the scan shows only minor issues, trust your gut. The AI is a tool to validate and clarify your intuition, not replace it. It may not catch every nuanced, real-world cue.
4.  **Confronting Someone with "AI Evidence":** Never say, "My AI app says you're manipulative." Use the insights to formulate your own observations and questions (e.g., "I've noticed a pattern where my opinions are often dismissed. Can we talk about that?").
5.  **Only Scanning "Problem" Conversations:** Try scanning a conversation that feels genuinely good and healthy. This gives you a baseline for what positive, respectful communication looks like through the AI's lens, making unhealthy patterns even clearer by contrast.
