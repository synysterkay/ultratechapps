### Step-by-Step Tutorial to Canine Weight Loss Success
Follow this structured 7-step guide to set your dog on the path to a healthier weight.

**Step 1: Commit to the "Why" and Set a Goal**
First, solidify your motivation. Write down why you want your dog to lose weight—is it for more energy, less joint pain, or a longer life together? Then, consult your veterinarian to determine a safe target weight and rule out any underlying medical conditions. This professional goal is your North Star.

**Step 2: Conduct a Full Lifestyle Audit**
For three days, write down everything your dog consumes: every kibble portion, treat, table scrap, and dental chew. Also, note their activity level. This audit reveals hidden calories and establishes a baseline. You'll likely be surprised by the total.

### Common Mistakes to Avoid on Your Dog's Weight Loss Journey
Avoid these pitfalls to ensure a safe and effective transformation:
1.  **Crash Dieting:** Drastically slashing calories is dangerous and can cause nutritional deficiencies. Always use a calculated, vet-approved deficit like the one PupShape provides.
2.  **Forgetting Treat Calories:** Those "just a little" treats add up fast. Always log every single treat in your tracker and factor them into the daily budget. Consider using low-calorie options like green beans or blueberries.
3.  **Guessing Portions:** Eyeballing food leads to overfeeding. You must use a digital scale for absolute accuracy, as recommended by the app's meal plan.
4.  **Neglecting Adjustments:** If weight loss stalls for 2-3 weeks, the plan needs tweaking. Don't just give up; use your tracker data to intelligently reduce calories or increase activity slightly.
5.  **Skipping the Vet:** An annual check-up is non-negotiable. This ensures the weight loss is due to your plan and not an undiagnosed health issue like hypothyroidism.
