### Common Mistakes (and How to Fix Them)

1. **Overboosting to 150%** – Pushing volume beyond the safe limit causes speaker rattling and distortion. Solution: Keep the Smart Boost slider between 100% and 130% for clear sound.

2. **Ignoring Equalizer Settings** – Many users only boost volume, leaving the equalizer flat. This results in muddy audio. Solution: Always apply a preset or custom EQ curve (like the one in Step 4).
