## Why It's So Hard to Tell the Difference

At the start, love bombing and genuine affection can look identical. Both involve excitement, gifts, and sweet words. The core difference lies in intent and sustainability. Genuine affection grows steadily and respects your boundaries. Love bombing is a tactic—an overwhelming flood of attention designed to create dependency and fast-track intimacy so that red flags are ignored. The emotional high makes it incredibly difficult to see the manipulation in real-time. That's where modern tools like **Red Flag Scanner AI** become essential.
