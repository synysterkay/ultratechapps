## Common Mistakes to Avoid

**Mistake 1: Not Testing Audio First.** Poor recording quality leads to inaccurate transcripts. Do a quick 10-second test before the real meeting starts to ensure **Smart Notes** is picking up clear audio from your conferencing app.

**Mistake 2: Skipping the Post-Meeting Review.** Blindly trusting the AI's first draft without a quick scan for context. Always spend two minutes reviewing the summary, especially on action item assignments, to catch any rare misinterpretations.

**Mistake 3: Hoarding the Notes.** The biggest value is in shared alignment. If you generate the brilliant summary but forget to share it, you've lost 80% of the benefit. Make sharing the final, non-negotiable step of your process.
