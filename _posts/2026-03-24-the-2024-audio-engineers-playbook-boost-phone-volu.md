## 5 Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

1.  **Mistake:** Cranking the booster dial to 100% immediately. **Solution:** This almost guarantees distortion and potential damage. Increase the booster level gradually, in 10% increments, while lowering your system volume to compensate.
2.  **Mistake:** Ignoring the equalizer and only using raw volume. **Solution:** Raw boosting amplifies flaws. Always use the EQ to shape the tone. The presets are a perfect starting point.
3.  **Mistake:** Testing on phone speakers. **Solution:** Phone speakers distort easily and can't reproduce low frequencies. Always use headphones for accurate tuning and to protect the speaker hardware.
4.  **Mistake:** Using the same profile for all audio. **Solution:** Create different profiles. Use a bass-heavy profile for music, the "Voice" preset for podcasts, and a balanced profile with 3D for movies.
5.  **Mistake:** Forgetting to lower system volume. **Solution:** The booster app works *with* your system volume. If you max both, you'll create a clipped, dangerous signal. Think of the system volume as the master, and the booster as a quality enhancer.
