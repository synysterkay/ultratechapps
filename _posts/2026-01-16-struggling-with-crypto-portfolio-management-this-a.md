This is the most critical yet commonly misunderstood metric. Your exchange shows your total balance, but what have you *actually* made or lost? **Crypto AI** automatically calculates your true **profit and loss**, separating paper gains (unrealized) from actual money in/out of your bank account (realized). ### 2. Portfolio Allocation & Diversification Score

Are you overexposed to one coin or sector? A sudden crash in Ethereum can devastate a portfolio that’s 70% ETH. The app provides a crystal-clear visual breakdown of your **asset allocation** across coins, sectors (DeFi, NFT, Layer 1, etc.), and even risk profiles. ### 3. Individual Asset Alpha (Is Your Pick Beating the Market?)

This is the secret metric most investors miss. Just because Bitcoin is up 10% doesn’t mean your altcoin pick was a good one. Alpha measures an asset’s performance *relative to a benchmark* (like BTC or ETH). **Crypto AI** calculates this for every coin you hold. Is your altcoin rising faster than Bitcoin, or is it just riding the market wave and actually underperforming? This metric tells you the true skill behind your picks.

### 4. Volatility & Risk Assessment

Not all coins carry the same risk. The app analyzes the historical **volatility** of each asset in your portfolio. It helps you identify your high-risk, high-reward moonshots versus your stable, core holdings. This allows for smarter **risk management**—you can decide if your portfolio’s overall risk level matches your sleep-at-night comfort zone.

This is the killer feature. The app’s AI doesn’t just show data; it interprets it.
