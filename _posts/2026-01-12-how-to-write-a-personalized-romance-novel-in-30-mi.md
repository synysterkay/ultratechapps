## Common Mistakes to Avoid in AI Storytelling

Even with a powerful tool, a few missteps can hinder your experience. Avoid these pitfalls:

1.  **Being Too Vague:** Inputting "a love story" gives the AI nothing to work with, leading to generic output. Always include a specific trope, setting, or character conflict.
2.  **Over-Editing Too Soon:** Let the AI generate a full scene or conversation before making tweaks. Constant micro-managing interrupts the narrative flow and can create inconsistencies.
3.  **Ignoring the Branching Choices:** The power of personalized storytelling lies in these decisions. Always make a choice that genuinely interests you, rather than picking randomly, to ensure the story remains compelling.
4.  **Forgetting Character Consistency:** If you decide your hero is afraid of heights in chapter one, don't have him casually scale a cliff in chapter three unless it's a deliberate character growth moment. Use the app's edit feature to maintain continuity.
