## Common Boundary-Setting Mistakes to Avoid

Even with the best intentions, it's easy to stumble. Avoid these pitfalls to stay on track:

1.  **The Digital "Just One Look" Trap:** Unfollowing or muting is not cruel; it's a critical boundary. Scrolling "just to see" reopens the wound every time. Use app blockers if needed, and redirect that impulse to your Fresh Start journal.
2.  **Bargaining with Your Own Rules:** "It's their birthday, so I can text" or "This one exception won't hurt." This undermines your entire plan. Consistency is key. Trust the process you built with your AI guide.
3.  **Isolating Yourself Completely:** Boundaries are about creating healthy space, not building a fortress. The **Fresh Start** app community or journaling can provide connection without the risk of emotional backsliding.
4.  **Expecting Instant Perfection:** You might slip up. That's normal. The mistake is using a slip-up as an excuse to abandon all boundaries. Use the app to process the setback and recommit immediately.
