## 5 Tips to Protect Your Hearing with Sound Boosters

### 1. Follow the 60/60 Rule

Listen at 60% of the maximum boost for 60 minutes max. I set a phone timer. This simple habit cut my hearing fatigue by half.

### 2. Use Over-Ear Headphones

In-ear buds can amplify sound more directly. Over-ear headphones provide a safer listening experience. I switched to over-ears on day 15 and noticed cleaner bass.

### 3. Avoid Boosting in Quiet Environments

In a silent room, you don't need extra volume. Save the boost for noisy places like subways or gyms. My usage dropped 30% once I stopped boosting at home.

### 4. Check Your Headphone Impedance

High-impedance headphones (over 32 ohms) need more power. **Volume Booster - Sound Booster** works best with standard earbuds and headphones. If you use studio monitors, start with 5% boost.

### 5. Listen for Distortion

If you hear crackling or buzzing, reduce the boost. That's your signal that the audio is clipping. I learned this the hard way on day 3—now I keep it clean.
