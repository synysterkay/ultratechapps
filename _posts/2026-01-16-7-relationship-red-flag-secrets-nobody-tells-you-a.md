## Why We Miss Red Flags: The Psychology of Attachment and Bias
Our brains are wired for connection, not always for clear-eyed analysis. Psychological concepts like **confirmation bias** (favoring information that confirms our existing hopes) and the **honeymoon phase** neurochemical cocktail (dopamine, oxytocin) actively blind us to potential problems. We rationalize, we make excuses, and we often ignore the subtle **toxic patterns** because we're invested in the story we want to be true.

This is where an external, objective tool becomes invaluable. Red Flag Scanner AI acts as a **cognitive buffer**, helping you separate genuine concern from anxiety, and identifying real **warning signs** from normal relationship friction.
