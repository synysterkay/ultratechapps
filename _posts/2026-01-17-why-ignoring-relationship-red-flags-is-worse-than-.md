## The Hidden Cost of Missed Red Flags: A Deep Dive

You've probably felt it—that nagging doubt in the pit of your stomach. Maybe your partner frequently criticizes you under the guise of "just being honest." The problem with **toxic relationship patterns** is their insidious nature.
