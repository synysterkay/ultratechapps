## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Maxing out the boost slider immediately.** This causes distortion and can damage speakers. Start at +10–15% and increase gradually.
2. **Ignoring the limiter.** The limiter prevents clipping — always keep it on unless you’re using external speakers.
3. **Using the same EQ for movies and games.** Movies benefit from bass, games from mids/highs. Use per-app profiles.
4. **Forgetting to test with headphones.** Speaker and headphone responses differ; what sounds great on speaker may be harsh on headphones.
5. **Not granting all permissions.** Without overlay and usage access, per-app profiles and floating controls won’t work.
