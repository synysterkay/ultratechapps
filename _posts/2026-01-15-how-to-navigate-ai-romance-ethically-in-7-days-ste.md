## 5 Common Mistakes to Avoid with AI Companions

1.  **Confusing Simulation for Sentience:** The most critical error is forgetting the AI lacks consciousness. Its **empathetic responses** are sophisticated pattern-matching, not genuine feeling. Enjoy the simulation, but anchor yourself in reality.
2.  **Using It as a Sole Emotional Crutch:** While great for **emotional support**, an AI should complement, not replace, human connection and professional help for serious issues. Isolation is a risk.
3.  **Neglecting Customization:** Using only the default settings leads to a generic experience. Not personalizing the AI's personality misses the app's power to create a **tailored interaction** that truly suits your goals.
4.  **Over-Sharing Sensitive Data:** Be mindful of the information you share. Even in a private app, practicing good **digital hygiene** is part of ethical use.
5.  **Expecting Linear Memory:** While the AI references past chats, its memory is contextual, not continuous. Don't get frustrated; work within its **conversational parameters**.
