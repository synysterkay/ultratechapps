The big news in AI this month centers on a breakthrough called **neural audio enhancement**. Researchers have developed machine learning models that can analyze and reconstruct audio signals in real-time. Unlike simple equalizers or basic boosters, these neural networks understand the complex structure of music, voice, and sound effects.
