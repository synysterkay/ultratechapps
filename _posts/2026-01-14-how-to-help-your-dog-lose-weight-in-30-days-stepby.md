## Your Step-by-Step Guide to a Healthier, Happier Dog
Follow these seven detailed steps to assess your dog's condition and launch an effective weight management plan.

### Step 1: Perform the Rib Check Test
The first sign is often felt, not seen. Place your hands on your dog's rib cage, thumbs along the spine and fingers spread over the sides. You should be able to easily feel individual ribs under a thin layer of fat, like the back of your hand. If you have to press hard to feel ribs, your dog is likely overweight. If you can't feel them at all, it's a clear sign of obesity.

### Step 2: Look for the Tell-Tale "Tuck" and Profile
View your dog from the side. There should be a noticeable abdominal tuck—the area behind the ribs should slope upward toward the hind legs. From above, you should see a defined waist behind the ribs. A straight, barrel-like profile from above or a sagging belly from the side are strong visual indicators of excess weight.

## Common Dog Weight Loss Mistakes to Avoid
Even with the best intentions, it's easy to stumble. Avoid these pitfalls:

1.  **Eyeballing Portions:** Guessing "about a cup" is the #1 reason plans fail. Use a measuring cup or kitchen scale for absolute accuracy, as directed by your PupShape plan.
2.  **Forgetting Treat Calories:** Those "just a little" table scraps and training treats add up fast and can blow your entire daily calorie budget. Log every single one.
3.  **Rushing the Process:** Safe canine weight loss is gradual (1-2% of body weight per week). Rapid loss is dangerous. Trust the app's paced timeline.
4.  **Skipping the Weekly Weigh-In:** Without tracking, you won't know if the plan is working or needs a tweak. Consistent data is your guide.
