## Step-by-Step: Your Weekly 30-Minute Date Planning Session

### Step 1: The Solo Brain Dump (5 Minutes)
**What to do:** Before you connect with your partner, open SoulPlan individually. Don't overthink it. For 5 minutes, simply scroll through the app's "Explore" or "Ideas" feed. Tap the heart icon on any date idea that gives you even a flicker of interest—a local hiking trail, a new board game cafe, a recipe for homemade pasta. The goal is quantity, not quality, to prime your creative pumps.
**What you'll see:** You'll build a private, initial list of liked ideas. SoulPlan's interface is visual and intuitive, making browsing feel like scrolling a personalized social feed for experiences.

**Common Error:** Judging ideas too harshly at this stage ("That's too expensive," "We did that last year"). Just save it.

### Step 2: Sync Up & Set the Budget Filter (3 Minutes)
**What to do:** Now, connect with your partner. Open SoulPlan and ensure you're both logged into your linked "Couple" profile. Navigate to the settings or filter icon. Here, you will set your most crucial parameter: the budget. Select a predefined range like **"$0-$25," "$26-$50," or "$51-$100+"**.
**What you'll see:** The entire app's suggestion engine now recalibrates. The idea feed will refresh, prioritizing options within your selected financial comfort zone.
**Common Error:** Not agreeing on the budget first. This prevents disappointment when one person falls in love with a $200 tasting menu on a $40 budget week.

### Step 3: Collaborative Idea Generation with Swipe & Match (10 Minutes)
**What to do:** This is SoulPlan's core feature. Enter the "Swipe" or "Match" section. You and your partner will be shown the same stream of date ideas, tailored to your budget. Independently, swipe RIGHT (like) or LEFT (pass) on each idea. The app works in the background to find your matches.
**What you'll see:** After a few minutes of swiping, SoulPlan will reveal a list of **"Matched Ideas"**—dates you both expressed interest in. This eliminates the "I don't know, what do *you* want to do?" loop and surfaces mutually exciting options.
**Common Error:** Swiping to please your partner. Be authentic! The magic happens in the overlap of your genuine interests.

### Step 4: Refine & Vote on Your Top 5 (7 Minutes)
**What to do:** Review your matched list. SoulPlan often provides 10-15 matches. Now, discuss and shortlist. Use the in-app voting or polling feature (often a star rating or upvote system) to rank your top five favorites for the week or month. I recommend naming this list something like **"May Adventure Queue"** or **"Summer 2024 Dates."**
**What you'll see:** A curated, prioritized shortlist of dates you're both genuinely excited about. The pressure of deciding *what* to do is now completely resolved for the foreseeable future.
**Common Error:** Trying to plan the exact day and time for all five dates now. Just sequence them in order of desire.

### Step 5: Schedule & Delegate with the Shared Calendar (5 Minutes)
**What to do:** Take your #1 ranked idea from the shortlist. In SoulPlan, tap to view its details, then use the **"Plan This Date"** or **"Add to Calendar"** function. Create a new event, set a tentative day and time for the upcoming week, and assign tasks. SoulPlan lets you split to-dos (e.g., Partner A: Book tickets; Partner B: Arrange babysitter).
**What you'll see:** The date is now on your app's shared calendar. Notifications will remind you both, and task assignments create clear accountability without nagging.
**Common Error:** Forgetting to use the task delegation. This feature is key to sharing the logistical load equitably.

## 5 Common Mistakes & How SoulPlan Helps You Avoid Them

1.  **The Budget Blindside:** Picking a date without considering cost leads to stress. **Solution:** Always use SoulPlan's budget filter *first*. It frames all suggestions in a financially realistic context.
2.  **The One-Sided Planner:** One person does all the research and emotional labor. **Solution:** The Swipe & Match feature *requires* input from both partners, making planning inherently collaborative and fair.
3.  **The Vague "Someday" Idea:** You have a great idea but never pin it down. **Solution:** The instant you match on an idea, use the "Plan This Date" button to draft it into your shared calendar with a placeholder time.
4.  **The Logistical Black Hole:** You agree on *what* but fail on *who does what*. **Solution:** Use the in-app task assignment feature when creating the calendar event. "You book, I'll drive" is decided on the spot.
5.  **The Rut Reinforcement:** Going back to the same three safe options. **Solution:** SoulPlan's algorithm constantly surfaces new and trending local ideas, pushing you gently outside your comfort zone based on your shared likes.
