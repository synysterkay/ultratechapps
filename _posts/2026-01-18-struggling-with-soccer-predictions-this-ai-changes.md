## Why Your Current Methods Are Setting You Up to Fail

We default to familiar tactics. Maybe you scour fan forums, trust a favorite influencer, or build complex spreadsheets with last season's data. These traditional solutions fail because they're reactive, not predictive. They look backward at what *has* happened, not forward at what *will* happen.

Human analysis is also plagued by bias. We overvalue a star player, ignore a team's poor underlying defensive metrics because they're on a winning streak, or get swayed by recent headlines. Manually tracking real-time factors like expected goals (xG) trends, pressing intensity changes, or a manager's tactical shift in cup competitions is a full-time job. It's no wonder you feel like you're always one step behind. The game has evolved into a data-rich ecosystem, but most fans are still trying to play it with a paper map.
