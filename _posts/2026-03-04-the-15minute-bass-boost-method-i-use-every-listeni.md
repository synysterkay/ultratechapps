## 5 Bass Boost Mistakes That Ruin Your Audio (And How to Avoid Them)

**Mistake #1: Maxing out all bass controls**
**Solution:** The "less is more" principle applies strongly here. Increases of 2-4dB per band are usually sufficient. Volume Booster - Sound Booster's 3D Bass feature does much of the heavy lifting intelligently.

**Mistake #2: Ignoring midrange frequencies**
**Solution:** When you boost bass significantly, you sometimes need to slightly reduce midrange (910Hz slider by -1dB) to maintain balance. This prevents the "muddy" sound where everything blends together.

**Mistake #3: Using the same settings for all content**
**Solution:** Create multiple profiles in Volume Booster - Sound Booster. Classical music needs different bass treatment than EDM. Save time by setting up genre-specific profiles once.

**Mistake #4: Boosting volume instead of bass**
**Solution:** If your audio lacks bass, increasing overall volume often makes it worse. Use Volume Booster - Sound Booster's EQ and 3D Bass features first, then apply modest volume boost if needed.

**Mistake #5: Not preventing distortion**
**Solution:** Always enable "Prevent Distortion" in Volume Booster - Sound Booster's settings. This uses intelligent limiting to protect your audio quality and your hearing.
