### My Testing Methodology: How I Evaluated These Audio Apps

I spent 40 hours over three weeks testing each app on a Google Pixel 7 and a Samsung Galaxy S22. I used high-quality FLAC files, Spotify Premium, and YouTube videos. I also considered battery drain, app stability, and ease of use. My goal was to find apps that not only boost volume but also provide meaningful equalization control.

### Key Criteria for Comparing Sound Booster and Equalizer Apps

To be fair, I evaluated each app on seven factors, weighted by importance:

1. **Sound Quality Enhancement (25%)**: How well does it improve clarity, detail, and depth?
2. **Volume Boost Capability (20%)**: Can it genuinely make audio louder without distortion?
3. **Equalizer Control (20%)**: Does it offer a full 10-band or more, with presets and customization?
4. **Ease of Use (15%)**: Is the interface intuitive for both beginners and experts?
5. **Compatibility (10%)**: Does it work with all streaming apps and local files?
6. **Battery and Resource Efficiency (5%)**: Does it drain your phone's battery excessively?
7. **Extra Features (5%)**: Does it offer extras like bass boost, virtualizer, or presets?
