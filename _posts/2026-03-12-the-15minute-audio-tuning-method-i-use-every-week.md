### 5 Common Equalizer Mistakes (And How to Avoid Them)

1.  **The "V" Shape Trap:** Boosting only bass and treble into a 'V' creates a hollow, scooped-out midrange, making vocals distant. **Solution:** Always make smaller, complementary adjustments to the mid bands (910Hz).
2.  **Overdriving the Preamp:** Aggressive boosting across multiple bands can cause digital clipping (distortion). **Solution:** If you boost multiple bands, consider slightly lowering the master gain or volume booster to compensate.
3.  **Ignoring Your Hardware:** Cheap earbuds may distort at certain frequencies. **Solution:** If you hear crackling at a specific boost, reduce that band. Tune for your specific headphones.
4.  **Setting and Forgetting:** One profile doesn't fit all music genres. **Solution:** Create and save separate profiles for Rock, Classical, Podcasts, and Movies.
