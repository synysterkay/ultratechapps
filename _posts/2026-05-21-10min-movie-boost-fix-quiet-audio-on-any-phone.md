## 5 Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them

1. **Over-boosting beyond 80%** – Causes distortion and speaker damage. Solution: Stay at 50-70% for most content. If still quiet, use EQ or compression instead.

2. **Forgetting to enable compression** – Without it, loud scenes remain loud, quiet ones stay quiet. Solution: Always toggle compression on for movies.

3. **Using wrong preset** – “Music” preset boosts bass, making movie dialogue muddy. Solution: Always select “Movie” or “Podcast” preset.

4. **Ignoring the visualizer** – Red spikes mean clipping, which ruins audio. Solution: Reduce boost until the visualizer stays green or yellow.

5. **Not saving profiles** – Manually adjusting settings every time wastes minutes. Solution: Save at least two profiles (one for movies, one for podcasts).
