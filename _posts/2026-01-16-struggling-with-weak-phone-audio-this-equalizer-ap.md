## Why Basic Volume Buttons Aren't the Answer

When the sound isn't right, what's your first instinct? You tap the volume up button. Again. And again. Until you see that warning message: "Listening at high volume can damage your hearing." You've hit the hard-coded limit. Pushing volume to the max is the old, brute-force approach. It doesn't *enhance* the sound; it just makes the flaws louder. It can distort the audio, causing harsh treble and muddy, unbalanced bass. It's like turning up a dim, yellow light instead of installing a bright, clear bulb. You're not solving the problem of poor sound quality; you're amplifying it, often at the risk of damaging your speakers or headphones over time. This traditional "solution" leaves you frustrated, with fatigued ears and a subpar listening experience.
