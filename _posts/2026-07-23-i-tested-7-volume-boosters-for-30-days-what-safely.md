## I Spent 30 Days Testing 7 Volume Boosters - Here's What Actually Works

You know that sinking feeling when your phone's volume maxes out and you still can't hear your favorite song over the subway rumble? I've been there too. After blowing out two phone speakers in 2023 by cranking third-party boosters, I decided to get scientific.

For 30 days, I tested 7 popular volume booster apps on a Samsung Galaxy S24 (fresh speakers, I promise). I measured decibel output with a calibrated dB meter, monitored battery drain with AccuBattery, and checked for distortion using audio analysis software. The goal: find which app can safely boost volume without damaging your speakers.

My criteria? **Safety** (40% weight) - no clipping or overheating; **Sound quality** (25%) - minimal distortion; **Battery impact** (15%); **Ease of use** (10%); **Extra features** (10%). Let's dive into the results.

## Comparison Criteria

I ranked each app across five weighted factors:

1. **Speaker Safety (40%)** - Does the app prevent clipping, limit gain to safe levels, and avoid overheating? Critical because most phone speakers handle only 1-2 watts continuous power.
2. **Sound Quality (25%)** - Measured as total harmonic distortion (THD) below 1% at maximum safe boost. Lower THD means cleaner audio.
3. **Battery Efficiency (15%)** - Percentage increase in battery drain during 1 hour of music playback at boosted volume.
4. **User Experience (10%)** - Setup time, clarity of controls, and accessibility.
5. **Extra Features (10%)** - Equalizer presets, bass boost, per-app settings, and volume limiter.
