### 2. Equalizer FX

Equalizer FX offers a 5-band EQ with 10 presets. In testing, it boosted volume by 25% (to 106dB) but introduced noticeable distortion at maximum gain. The bass boost added 8dB at 80Hz, but the treble range (8-16kHz) was muddy. It works with most streaming apps but requires a separate permission for each app.

### 3. Bass Booster & Volume Booster

This app focuses on bass but lacks a full EQ. It boosted volume by 30% (to 110dB) and added 10dB at 60Hz. However, the lack of treble control made rock and classical music sound boxy. Setup was quick (30 seconds), but it drains battery faster (12% per hour vs. 8% for Volume Booster).

### 4. Music Volume EQ

Music Volume EQ has a 7-band EQ with 15 presets. Volume boost reached 112dB (35% increase), but the app frequently crashed when switching between genres. The classical preset was decent (within 3dB of ideal), but the hip-hop preset over-boosted bass by 18dB, causing distortion.

### 5. Powerful Equalizer

Powerful Equalizer offers a 10-band EQ and 20 presets. Volume boost was 32% (to 114dB). Bass boost added 13dB at 60Hz, and treble accuracy was good. However, the interface is cluttered with ads, and setup took 2 minutes due to complex menus.
