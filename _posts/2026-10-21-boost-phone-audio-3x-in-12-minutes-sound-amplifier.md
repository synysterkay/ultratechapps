### Step 2: Set Your Baseline Volume
Before boosting, play a video or game at your phone's normal maximum volume. Note how it sounds. This gives you a reference point. In the app, ensure the booster is off initially. You'll see a decibel meter showing current output.

### Step 3: Activate the Booster
Toggle the main switch to 'On'. You'll immediately hear a difference. The app's default boost is +50%. You can adjust the slider from 0% to 200%. Start at 50% to avoid distortion. What you'll see: A green level indicator that moves with the audio. Common error: If you hear crackling, reduce the boost level.

### Step 4: Fine-Tune with the Equalizer
Tap the equalizer icon. You'll see a 5-band EQ. For movies, boost the mid and high frequencies to hear dialogue clearly. For gaming, enhance lows for explosions and highs for footsteps. Use the preset 'Movie' or 'Game' for quick setup. I personally use the 'Movie' preset when watching Netflix—it makes dialogues crisp.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Over-amplifying beyond 150%**: This can damage speakers. Stick to 100-150% for most devices.
2. **Ignoring the equalizer**: The EQ is crucial for clarity. Don't just max out volume.
3. **Using max bass boost with small speakers**: Causes distortion. Use bass boost moderately.
4. **Not testing with different content**: Settings for movies may not work for games. Create presets.
5. **Forgetting to disable when not needed**: Running the booster constantly drains battery. Use auto-start wisely.
