## Common Mistakes to Avoid for Success

Avoiding these pitfalls will smooth your path to success:

1.  **Eyeballing Portions:** This is the #1 reason plans fail. A few extra kibbles each day can add hundreds of extra calories weekly. Always use a measuring cup or a kitchen scale for absolute accuracy, as guided by your PupShape plan.
2.  **Forgetting About Treats:** Those training treats and table scraps count! Log every single treat in the app. Consider using low-calorie options like green beans or carrots, and always deduct them from the daily calorie budget.
3.  **Inconsistent Weigh-Ins:** Weigh your dog at the same time of day, on the same scale, once a week. Skipping weigh-ins means you're flying blind and can't make timely adjustments.
4.  **Expecting Overnight Results:** Healthy weight loss is gradual (1-2% of body weight per week). Patience and consistency with your logged plan are far more important than rapid, unsustainable drops.
5.  **Neglecting Exercise:** Diet is primary, but exercise builds muscle, boosts metabolism, and improves mental health. Integrate consistent, gentle activity into your daily log.
