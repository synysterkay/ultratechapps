The first step is acceptance. Your brain is literally going through withdrawal. Studies show that romantic rejection activates the same neural pathways as physical pain. ## Common Mistakes That Slow Down Healing

Avoid these pitfalls to stay on the fastest path to recovery.

*   **Isolating Yourself:** Withdrawing from friends and family amplifies pain. Force social contact, even when you don't feel like it. Use the app's community features or insights to feel connected without pressure.
*   **Stalking Your Ex on Social Media:** This is digital self-harm. It re-triggers pain and prevents **emotional detachment**. The app offers strategies to resist this urge and refocus your attention.
*   **Rushing into a Rebound Relationship:** Using a new person as a distraction is a classic **avoidance behavior**. It halts your internal processing. The app helps you distinguish between loneliness and genuine readiness.
*   **Bottling Up Emotions:** Pretending you're "fine" prolongs suffering. The AI journal in Fresh Start provides a safe, private space to release these feelings without burdening others or facing judgment.
